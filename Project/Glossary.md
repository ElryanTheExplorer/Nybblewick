# Glossary

- **Nybble** (nibble): 4 bits, one hex digit, one hand shape. See [[Chorded Nybble]].
- **Chord**: fingers held together as one input.
- **Commit**: locking a chord in. See [[Thumb Commit]], [[Cast Trigger Modes]].
- **Address**: row chord + column chord, e.g. `C7`. See [[Two-Chord Addressing]].
- **Origin cell**: the cell you chord to fire a spell. See [[Polyomino Spells]].
- **Socket**: a requirement on a spell's border, filled by an adjacent component. See [[Sockets and Components]].
- **Component**: a foraged, tagged, shaped piece placed on the board. See [[Components]].
- **Wild magic**: an out-of-range column that warps rather than fails. See [[Wild Magic Overshoot]].
- **Finger cost**: popcount(row) + popcount(column). See [[Finger Cost]].
- **Popcount**: number of 1 bits (open fingers).
- **Familiar**: a companion with its own vision. See [[Familiar Vision]].
- **Plate**: one of six inventory concepts. See [[Inventory Plates]].
- **MOC**: map of content, an index note.

## Pass 2 terms (2026-11-14)
See [[Canon Sheet]] for places, orders and shared terms (Standing, Notoriety, Oath, the Hollow, the Ring).

- **Afterglow**: faint trace of another player's solving chord at a puzzle.
- **Apprentice marks**: 8 4 2 1 inked on the knuckles.
- **Astral projection**: leaving the body to scout the spirit layer; view and mark, never touch.
- **Bit rings**: rings that glow when their finger is open.
- **Blight Gambit**: open, contest-legal Necro move turning a rival's Bloom offering to Rot (compostable back).
- **Body ping**: 5 s warning that something is about to happen to your body.
- **Breath**: the projection's time limit.
- **Cast beat**: a story-scene choice made by casting: hand close-up, then wide shot of the result.
- **Catalyst**: rare component that changes a spell's behavior, not power; applies only touching the origin cell.
- **Divided-loyalty scene**: a flashpoint between your origin and your splash school.
- **Duel tempo**: agreed response window: Slow 10 s, Measured 6 s, Quick 3 s.
- **Dye ramp**: palette ramp swapped in by a dye.
- **Fainting**: the only failure state; wake at the last waystone with everything carried.
- **Fair swap**: fixed NPC barter rate.
- **Feed / Check**: a school supports its clockwise ring neighbor / restrains the school two steps ahead.
- **Flashpoint**: a borderland scene played in both neighbor orders' storylines.
- **Foothold**: a 2.5D spot where you stand still and may chord.
- **Footprint**: a spell's polyomino shape (I2, L3, O4, T4).
- **Fumble branch**: authored outcome of a failed QTE; the story continues.
- **Ghost**: outline of a missing spell or component in a preset or code.
- **Glyph message**: async message written as a chord address (row = template, column = word).
- **Lantern**: a wizard's personal light, which shows Notoriety: Clear, Smoking, Guttering, Snuffed.
- **Hearth Rule**: host session setting for allowed conflict: Peaceful (default), Mischief, Cutpurse.
- **Keepsake**: component a guest takes home, with provenance of the place they helped restore.
- **Ley line**: region-to-Hollow link delivering daily parcels of basic components.
- **Lining**: inside of the cloak; the board's face.
- **Loadout code**: hex string encoding a layout, ending in an XOR check digit.
- **Mark**: duel scoring unit (tether on an unwarded cell); three Marks win.
- **Order rites**: Act III branches only a devoted (mono-school) apprentice can take.
- **Page**: one board in the grimoire; pages share one component layer in the cloak. Other grimoire items are **leaves** (flowchart leaf, duel leaf, scratch leaf).
- **Patch**: 4-cell block of pockets; the unit the cloak grows in.
- **Pocket**: a sewn cloak cell; usable only if reachable by fingers and sewn.
- **Preset**: a saved layout, swapped at waystones or the sanctum.
- **Provenance shelf**: sanctum display of labelled components.
- **Reach**: cells your unlocked fingers can address.
- **Region moods**: the Drift (Glasshigh), the Sour (Saltings), the Grey Blight (Tanglewood), the Silence (Barrowfen), the Overcharge (Ironreach), the Severing (Crossings). See [[Ring Regions]].
- **Rehearsal / Rewrite**: replay a branch with no consequences / rewind for real within the current chapter.
- **Relighting**: redemption ritual at Nybblewick lowering Notoriety one step.
- **Remnant tongue**: (P2) in-world name for chords.
- **Restoration stages**: Blighted, Stirring, Mending, Thriving, Restored.
- **Rooted casting**: you can't move while a chord is in progress.
- **Scratch page**: Research Loop experiment board; casts there use nothing.
- **Seats**: ritual raid roles (Anchor, Caller, Weaver, Reader); encounters scale by seats.
- **Shared Byte**: one spell cast by two players: one chords the row, the other the column.
- **Spark**: Smith power made by the Forge, banked, spent by bound spells.
- **Bound spell** (formerly standing spell): a spell chorded once into a station socket that keeps running on Spark.
- **Storied**: top quality tier: provenance NPCs recognise.
- **Tether (Wayfarer)**: line between two Lodestone anchors carrying the player or components. (Astral Projection also uses "tether" for its range limit.)
- **Tether**: range limit between projection and body.
- **the Dimming**: (P2) the event that ended great spells.
- **the Turning**: one-time late-game scene to re-pledge to another order.
- **the Wick**: the ancient lamp at the heart of Nybblewick that answers chords; lit dim in the prologue, full only at the end.
- **Two-keys rule**: every verb gate opens with at least two schools' verbs, or a verb plus a rune.
- **Waydoor**: fixed door pairing an order's seat with Nybblewick; opens when that region is restored.
- **Waystone**: region rest site: fast travel and loadout swaps.
- **Fuse**: the QTE timer, shown as a burning fuse.
- **Wound / heart**: a restoration site / a region's ritual boss.
- **Strain**: builds while you stay inside a hazard; full Strain means you faint (the only failure state).
- **Contest season**: a shared, real-world season for community contests, separate from personal in-game seasons.
- **Furniture**: sanctum fixtures (Loom, Wardrobe, lectern, dye vat, practice candle) that generate nothing and don't count toward the station cap.
