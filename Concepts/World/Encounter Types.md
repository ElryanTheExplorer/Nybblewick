# Encounter Types

What you face if it isn't combat (D-21, D-27). Each encounter is a situation to **read, prepare for and resolve** (the encounter layer of the [[Loop Stack]]). This is the proposed answer to **Q-22**.

## Shared rules
- Every encounter can be **read** before it acts: [[Astral Projection]], [[Familiar Vision]], or just looking.
- Every encounter has **at least two solutions** from different schools.
- Failure costs time, components or Standing. It never ends the run ([[Not Doing]]).
- **Strain and fainting:** hazards build **Strain** while you stay inside them (a vent cloud, a storm strike zone, sinking ground). Strain drains away once you step out. Full Strain = you **faint** and wake at the last waystone with everything you carried. Nothing else causes fainting; there is no health bar.
- No speed demand before fluency (D-20). Timed parts slow down or pause while you shape a chord unless you opt in to **Tight** timing. Choir windows are always at least 4 s.
- Resolution leaves a mark: a lit lamp, a mended bridge, a spirit that speaks again ([[Restoration Loop]]).

## Types
| Type | Example | Typical chords | Solo | Co-op |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hazard** | vent cloud, sinking ground, storm strike | 1-3 casts: neutralize, bridge, Lightning rod | read the pattern, cast at the gap before Strain fills | one player holds a ward while another crosses |
| **Blight front** | the grey blight's edge, brine creep spreading tile by tile | 5-15 casts over 2-5 min, pushing the front back | prepare bound spells as anchors, then work the edge | split the front; each player takes a flank |
| **Wary spirit / bargain** | the brine spirit, a road-spirit asking a story as toll, a dead voice that lost its name | 1-4 "spoken" casts, plus a gift of components ([[Provenance]] matters) | dialogue with casts as answers ([[Story Scene Loop]]) | one player speaks, others offer; spirits react to the group |
| **Broken infrastructure** | dead lift, washed-out ferry, cracked anvil | 3-6 casts in order: Weld, Charge, test | a small [[Rune Puzzles\|rune puzzle]] plus casts | parallel: one powers, one repairs |
| **Guardian** | an iron sentinel, a barrow warden-spirit | a lock to solve, not a foe to beat: power it down, prove a right, remember its maker | sneak, solve or bargain | one distracts with Glamour, one solves |
| **Ritual (boss)** | the Old Orrery, the Lightning Spire, the Long Naming | 15-40 casts over 5-15 min in phases | longer windows; bound spells hold anchors | [[Choir Casting]]; seats per phase ([[Ritual Raid Loop]]) |

## Bosses as rituals
A boss is a **force or a wound**, not an enemy: a storm, a broken machine, a silent barrow, a blight's heart. You don't kill it, you **resolve** it: calm, mend, lay to rest, bargain.

Shape of a ritual (example: **the storm at the Lightning Spire**, [[Ring Regions]]; spells from [[Smith Spellbook]]):
1. **Learn** (before): scout the spire, read the storm's rhythm (strikes every 3 counts), find the three iron anchor points.
2. **Prepare** (before): bring Struck lodestone, Copper coil and Iron nail; set a loadout preset with Lightning rod, Charge and Tether ([[Loadout Presets]]).
3. **Anchor** (phase 1): raise a Lightning rod at each anchor. Bound spells keep them up.
4. **Channel** (phase 2): each strike is a [[Chord QTEs|chord prompt]]; caught strikes go where the Act III choice sends them, missed ones spill into the ridge (it gets wilder and adds Strain nearby, nothing worse).
5. **Close** (phase 3): one long cast, chord by chord, to bind the storm.

**The outcome follows the [[Smith Storyline]] Act III choice:** (a) *harvest*: the storm feeds the Anvil Hall and Hollow stations ([[Automation]] gains Spark), the Stormline stays dangerous; (b) *ground*: storms calm, Smith loses that power; (c) *share*: a storm-road with the Waymarch along the Stormline. How well you cast grades the result within that choice (all three anchors held = the full version; fewer = it works, but the ridge stays stormy for a season).

**Q-53:** can a ritual be failed outright (the boss stays until you return), or is every attempt a graded resolution with consequences? Lean: graded, with one "it got away" outcome that returns next season.

## Solo versus co-op
- Solo players get bound spells to hold one task while they do another, and windows about 1.5× longer.
- Co-op splits seats (Anchor, Caller, Weaver, Reader), so harder chords become coordination rather than reflex ([[Choir Casting]]).
- Nothing requires a group. Some rituals have an optional co-op-only best outcome.

**Recommendation:** adopt the six types above as the whole PvE vocabulary. Rituals are the bosses; player-vs-player stays in [[Formal Duels]] and [[Subterfuge]].
