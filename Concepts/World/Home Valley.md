# Home Valley

**The Hollow**: the valley around Nybblewick at the center of the Ring. It is the persistent sandbox: you build here freely, and it keeps everything you do.

## At the start
- **Nybblewick**, a small wick-town. At its heart burns **the Wick**, the ancient lamp, lit dim in the prologue. Half the town's other lamps are dark; lighting them is your first lesson ([[Light-Up Drill]] as apprentice work).
- **Your plot**: a tumbledown sanctum (one room, one hearth, one empty station footing) on about 24×24 tiles of rough ground.
- **A stream** running through the valley from the Crossings side; a stand of woods toward the Tanglewood; a scree slope toward the Ironreach.
- **Six old roads** out to the [[Ring Regions]], all sealed except your school's.
- **A few townsfolk** who post small jobs ([[Commission Loop]]); placeholder roles: lamplighter, miller, ferryhand.

## What you build
- **Stations**, one per school ([[Sanctum Stations]]): garden, still, observatory, forge, waystation, ossuary. Furniture (loom, wardrobe, lectern, dye vat, practice candle) sits alongside and generates nothing.
- **Bound spells and tethers** that link them ([[Automation]]).
- **Land**: fields, ponds, channels, hedges, lit paths ([[Terraforming]]). The Hollow has no terraforming budget except plot space.
- **Roads and doors** out to the regions ([[Transport Network]]).
- **Looks**: everything visual about the sanctum is [[Sanctum Decor]].

## How it grows
| Trigger | What changes in the Hollow |
|---|---|
| First station built | Plot expands to 32×32 tiles |
| Each region reaches stage 2 ([[Restoration Loop]]) | A member of that order settles in town; their road becomes a lit, paved road |
| Each region restored | A fixed Waydoor to the order's seat opens in town; the order's stall appears at the market |
| Three regions restored | Order stalls stay open every day, not just in Fair week |
| All six restored | Plot expands to 48×48; the Wick burns at full strength (the end-game story beat, [[Premise Options]]) |

## Why you return
- **Harvest and reset**: stations fill while you travel ([[Sanctum Loop]]).
- **Prepare**: the research desk ([[Research Loop]]) and the loadout table ([[Loadout Presets]]).
- **People**: commissions, order members, story scenes that happen in town.
- **Hosting**: the Hollow is where friends drop in ([[Drop-In Co-op]]).
- **The Wick Fair**: one real-world Fair week at the end of each contest season, held on the town green ([[Seasonal Contest Loop]], [[Day Night and Seasons]]). Missing it costs nothing.
- **Seeing progress**: the valley is the visible record of the whole game ([[Design Pillars]] 2).

## Rules
- The Hollow has no hostile moods and no Strain. Weather and seasons affect it; blight never reaches it.
- Nothing in the Hollow decays while you're away ([[Anti-Chore Rules]]).

**Recommendation:** hand-build the valley's layout (town, stream, roads) and leave the plot open. A procedural Hollow adds replay value but loses the authored town that story scenes need.
