# Ring Regions

The six regions and six borderlands around the Hollow, in [[Canon Sheet]] ring order: **Astral → Alchemy → Bloom → Necro → Smith → Wayfarer → back to Astral**. Each region has a **mood** (its problem, from [[The Land's Moods]], matching its school's storyline), landmarks, signature encounters, sealed places and a restored state. Components named here are the Canon examples; the full list is in [[Component Catalog]]. Spell names are from the school spellbooks.

Every region is reachable from the Hollow by one old road, sealed at the start ([[Traversal and Verb Gates]]). Each seal can be opened by at least two schools' verbs.

## Regions

### The Glasshigh (Astral)
- **Biome:** high moors, observatory ruins, clear night skies.
- **Mood, the Drift:** the stars are drifting out of their charts. Nybblewick's star-timed lamps light late or not at all, and moor travellers following the old star-paths get lost ([[Astral Storyline]]).
- **Landmarks:** the **Starwatch Observatory** (the order's seat); **the Old Orrery**, a ruin reached by the **Orrery Stair**; the Cairn of Nine Lamps; the Mirror Tarn, a lake that shows the sky as it was.
- **Signature encounters:** re-charting a stretch of moor by night (Skychart); star-led paths that loop back on themselves; star-spirits who bargain for a constellation redrawn; **ritual:** rebuilding the Old Orrery over one night ([[Encounter Types]]).
- **Sealed:** crags (Lodestep, Root bridge or Star-step); the Old Orrery's seal (a [[Gray Code Locks|Gray code lock]]); the Tarn's reflection, walkable only at full moon.
- **Restored:** depends on the storyline's Act III (true sky, held illusion, or found cause). In every version the moor road's lamps keep time again, night maps reveal the region, and Starglass and Moonsilver nodes appear.

### The Saltings (Alchemy)
- **Biome:** salt marsh, hot springs, sulfur vents.
- **Mood, the Sour:** springs turned acrid; vents breathe poison clouds; brine creeps into fields.
- **Landmarks:** the Retort guild-house on stilts; the Boiling Stair (terraced springs); the Pan Fields (salt pans).
- **Signature encounters:** vent clouds to neutralize; corroded sluice gates (Weld); a brine spirit who trades in reagents; **ritual:** rebalancing the Boiling Stair, spring by spring.
- **Sealed:** salt crusts (dissolve, or crack with Kindle); deep bog (Root bridge, Brine causeway, or Ferry); the guild vault (a [[Parity Inscriptions|parity inscription]]).
- **Restored:** clear springs, bathers and pan-workers return, salt pans worked, more Fine and Storied Salt pinch and Retort.

### The Tanglewood (Bloom)
- **Biome:** old forest, overgrowth.
- **Mood, the Grey Blight:** Rot that nobody tended spills out of the Mulch. Leaves go soft and pale overnight and the grey edge creeps deeper each day ([[Bloom Storyline]]).
- **Landmarks:** the Hedge Circle grove; the Elder Stump; the Grey Edge, the blight's moving front.
- **Signature encounters:** holding the grey edge (replant, graft, move seedlings); a wary dryad who wants a glade left wild; a fallen bridge-tree; **ritual:** the storyline's Act III at the Grey Edge (restore the Rot cycle with the Wardens, cut a firebreak, or raise a hedge-wall).
- **Sealed:** blighted thickets (Wither or Kindle); the canopy (Root bridge); hollow roots (Wisp lantern to enter).
- **Restored:** the grey recedes, glades open, growth and decay balance; Mugwort and Moss mat are Fine more often here.

### The Barrowfen (Necro)
- **Biome:** fens, barrows, peat.
- **Mood, the Silence:** the dead have gone quiet. Ancestors no longer answer at the barrow doors because they have forgotten their own names, so Rot isn't raised and the Mulch overflows ([[Necro Storyline]]).
- **Landmarks:** the Barrow-hall (the Barrow Wardens); the Drowned Causeway; the Lantern Mounds; the worn name-seals on the barrow doors.
- **Signature encounters:** coaxing one old voice back; re-reading worn name-seals ([[Parity Inscriptions]], [[Serial Spirits]]); sinking ground; smouldering peat; **ritual:** the Long Naming, a vigil at the Lantern Mounds.
- **Sealed:** barrow doors (a rune, or a name remembered); the Drowned Causeway (Ferry, Drain, or Remembered path).
- **Restored:** lanterns lit on the mounds, the dead speak again and become story voices, Rot is raised and returned so the blight eases in the Mulch.

### The Ironreach (Smith)
- **Biome:** iron hills, storm ridges.
- **Mood, the Overcharge:** storms over the ridges have grown stronger; the iron in the hills holds their charge and won't let it go, so the forges overheat and crack ([[Smith Storyline]]).
- **Landmarks:** the Anvil Hall forge-town and its Bell Tower; the Lightning Spire; Senna's rod-field; the Old Lift into the deep mine.
- **Signature encounters:** storm fronts (Lightning rod); cracked anvils and a cracked bell to mend (Weld, Temper); an iron guardian that sleeps until powered; **ritual:** the storm at the Lightning Spire, whose outcome follows the storyline's Act III choice ([[Encounter Types]]).
- **Sealed:** storm-fused doors (Kindle or a rune); the storm ridge (Tether across); the deep mine (Wisp lantern plus Charge).
- **Restored:** forges run without cracking; lifts run; the storm is harvested, grounded or shared, per Act III.

### The Crossings (Wayfarer)
- **Biome:** river plains, old roads, ferries.
- **Mood, the Severing:** floods washed out the roads, ferries stopped, signposts point wrong; villages are cut off from each other.
- **Landmarks:** the Waymarch ferry-station; the Long Bridge (broken mid-span); the Milestone Circle.
- **Signature encounters:** flood fronts; a stranded ferry; lost caravans; a road-spirit that takes a story as toll; **ritual:** re-tying the Milestone Circle so roads lead true.
- **Sealed:** rivers (Ferry, Tether, or ice in Winter); the Long Bridge (Weld or Root bridge); the Circle's doors (Waydoor).
- **Restored:** ferries run on their own, roads link villages, and the Waymarch can teach player-placed Waydoors ([[Transport Network]]).

## Borderlands
Shared edges between neighbors. Smaller (about a third of a region), mixed moods, and the only steady source of dual components ([[Hybrid Spells]]).

| Borderland | Pair | Landscape | Signature encounter | Dual (Canon) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starsalt Flats | Astral + Alchemy | white salt flat that mirrors the night sky | read star-charts in the salt to find buried pans | Starsalt |
| Fungal Springs | Alchemy + Bloom | warm pools ringed with fungus | balance a spring that grows or poisons depending on the brew | Tincture moss |
| The Mulch | Bloom + Necro | leaf-litter forest floor, half alive; source of the grey blight | a blight front both schools must tend together | Ghostcap |
| Grave Forges | Necro + Smith | old smithies built into barrows | wake a dead smith's forge by remembering the smith | Grave iron |
| Stormline | Smith + Wayfarer | wind-scoured ridge with iron waymarks | ride the storm along tethers between waymarks ([[Storm]], Q-04) | Struck lodestone |
| Star Roads | Wayfarer + Astral | ancient roads aligned to stars | walk a road that only exists under its constellation | Compass glass |

## Proposals
- **Your school's region opens first.** The others open in any order; verb gates suggest but don't force it.
- Each region holds about 5 waystones (rest, loadout swap, fast travel), 10-14 restoration sites and one heart ritual ([[Restoration Loop]]).
- Region palettes follow the school ([[Art Direction]] open thread): Glasshigh blue-violet, Saltings white-ochre, Tanglewood green-umber (grey where blighted), Barrowfen grey-amber, Ironreach rust-slate, Crossings straw-teal.
