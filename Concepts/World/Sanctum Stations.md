# Sanctum Stations

**Purpose:** the "something that generates resources" from the brainstorm, and half of "wizard setup" (**Q-26**; the other half is the loadout, [[Loadout Presets]] and [[Cloak Pocket Progression]]). Stations in the Hollow grow basic components while you travel.
**Inputs:** a little school input, Spark, time spent playing.
**Outputs:** basic components (Plain quality, provenance "grown in your Hollow"), Spark.
**Player verbs / chords:** build, upgrade, inscribe bound spells ([[Automation]]).

This note owns the **numbers**. Looks are [[Sanctum Decor]]. Component names follow [[Component Catalog]].

## Stations
Rates are per in-game day (20 real minutes, [[Day Night and Seasons]]). Tiers 1 / 2 / 3.

| Station | School | Inputs per day | Output per day | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Garden** | Bloom | water (stream or tether); seed stock of 1 Bloom component | Mugwort 2 / 3 / 4; **Twine** 2 / 3 / 4 (fiber beds, spun on site); tier 3 adds Moss mat 1 | spring ×1.5, winter ×0.5 |
| **Still** | Alchemy | 1 Mugwort or Salt pinch; 4 Spark or 1 Hearth coal | Salt pinch 2 / 3 / 4 (full charges); **Spring vapor flask** – / 1 / 2 (bottled from the Hollow's spring); tier 3 adds Retort 1 every 2 days | — |
| **Observatory** | Astral | clear night (produces only at night); 3 Spark for lens tracking at tier 2+ | Starglass 2 / 3 / 4 | Moonsilver 1 / 1 / 2 on full-moon nights only |
| **Forge** | Smith | 1 Iron nail or scrap; fuel | Iron nail 1 / 2 / 3; Hearth coal 1 / 1 / 2; **Copper coil** – / 1 / 1 (wound from ridge copper); **Spark 6 / 12 / 20** | power source for [[Automation]] |
| **Waystation** | Wayfarer | needs one region at stage 2+ ([[Restoration Loop]]) | Lodestone 1 / 2 / 3; **parcel** of 3 / 5 / 8 basic components from a linked region you choose | hub of ley lines ([[Transport Network]]) |
| **Ossuary** | Necro | Rot you choose to compost | Grave-wax 2 / 3 / 4, 1 Rot each | takes Garden overflow marked for compost |

Twine, Spring vapor flask and Copper coil are the three commons [[Component Catalog]] lists as grown at the sanctum; they come from the Garden, the Still and the Forge.

## Furniture (not stations)
The **Loom**, **Wardrobe**, **lectern** (research desk), **dye vat** and **practice candle** are furniture. They don't count toward the 6-station cap and **generate nothing**. Their uses live in [[Research Loop]], [[Light-Up Drill]] and the Customization notes ([[Sanctum Decor]], [[Component Dyes]], [[Wizard Drip]]).

## Caps
- **Stations:** 6, one per school.
- **Storage:** 6 in-game days of output per station (about 2 hours of play, roughly three sessions, per [[Anti-Chore Rules]] rule 3). A full station stops producing; nothing is lost.
- **Share target:** stations supply at most about 40% of a player's *basic* components late game, and **0%** of duals, rares and Fine or Storied pieces. Foraging stays the source of quality and [[Provenance]].
- Build order is free. All six can be built; tier 3 everywhere is end-game.

## Limits and anti-chore rules
Must obey [[Anti-Chore Rules]]:
- **Stations run on play time.** Offline, they are frozen: no output, no decay, no loss.
- **Stored items never spoil.** Bloom freshness starts when you take a component out. Garden overflow becomes Rot only if you choose to compost it.
- **No daily timers, no streaks.** Coming back after a month gives the same station state as coming back the next day.
- **Q-52:** should offline time add a small trickle as a welcome back? Lean: no. It turns stations into a daily login reason.

## Works with Q-01
- **Casts consume:** stations are a steady basic supply that saves foraging time.
- **Casts only require:** stations matter for Spark, Twine for filler sockets, gifts ([[Gift and Provenance Economy]]), commissions, and packing several loadouts at once.

**Recommendation:** six stations, three tiers, play-time only, 6-day caps. Tie tier 3 to restoration so the Sanctum shows the Ring's progress.
