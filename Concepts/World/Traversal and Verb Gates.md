# Traversal and Verb Gates

How you move, and how the world opens. Metroidvania-style: places are sealed until you have the verb, and once opened they stay open as shortcuts. Spell names come from the spellbooks ([[Wayfarer Spellbook]], [[Bloom Spellbook]], [[Astral Spellbook]] and the others).

## Moving
| Move | Spell (school) | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk / run | — | everywhere | roads ×1.5 ([[Transport Network]]) |
| Hop | **Lodestep** (Wayfarer) | hop up to 4 tiles over gaps or up ledges | early utility verb; no aiming speed needed ("Blink" is the old prototype name) |
| Cross water | **Ferry** (Wayfarer) | ride mended ferries | runs by itself once mended |
| Zipline | **Tether** (Wayfarer) | line between two anchors | up to 24 tiles |
| Climb or bridge | **Root bridge** (Bloom) | grow a root bridge or vine ladder | lasts until cut; persists in the Hollow |
| Raise ground | **Upheave** (Bloom) | lift a low path out of a flood, shift a stream | a terraforming cast ([[Terraforming]]) |
| Light bridge | **Star-step** (Astral) | a short bridge of light | night only |
| Wade or descend | **Steep** (Alchemy) | a draught for crossing bog or descending safely | one draught per crossing |
| Drain | **Drain** (Alchemy) | draw off water and brine from a passage | stays drained in restored areas |
| Powered lift | **Charge** (Smith) | power a dead lift or rail | stays powered once restored |
| Old ways | **Remembered path** (Necro) | follow the dead's old way across a gap or causeway; holds for one in-game hour | becomes permanent once its story is heard |

Every move is a deliberate cast, never a reflex test (D-20). Gaps that need Lodestep are always safe to fail: you land back where you started.

## Verb gates
A **gate** is a seal that a verb opens. The rule: **every gate has at least two keys** from different schools, or one school key plus a rune ([[Rune Puzzles]]), so a single-school player is never stuck ([[Origins and Splashing]]).

| Gate | Keys |
|---|---|
| Blighted thicket | Wither (Necro) or Kindle (Smith) |
| Collapsed bridge | Weld (Smith), Root bridge (Bloom), or Remembered path (Necro) |
| Flooded passage | Drain (Alchemy), Ferry (Wayfarer), Upheave (Bloom), or ice in Winter |
| Dark cave | Wisp lantern (Astral) or Hearthstone (Smith) |
| Storm-fused door | Kindle (Smith) or a [[Logic Gate Runes\|logic-gate rune]] |
| Sealed vault | a rune ([[Gray Code Locks]], [[Parity Inscriptions]], [[PCB Seals]]) |
| High ledge | Lodestep (Wayfarer), Root bridge (Bloom), or Star-step at night (Astral) |
| Drifted road (star-paths that loop) | Skychart (Astral) or Lay road (Wayfarer) |
| Illusion or ward | Unweave (Astral) or a bargain |
| Spirit's threshold | a bargain ([[Encounter Types]]), or a name remembered ([[Necro Spellbook]]) |
| Stuck door | Waydoor (Wayfarer) or Weld (Smith) |

- Gates read as their key: thickets look grey and rotten, storm-fused doors crackle. No hidden logic.
- Rune gates are the [[Progression Axes]] Rune axis in the world: knowledge opens them, not components.

## Shortcuts that persist
- Opened gates stay open for good. One-way doors open from the far side, as in Metroidvanias.
- Each region has 3-5 shortcuts back to its first waystone, so a return trip takes about 5 minutes.
- Shortcuts opened by a co-op guest stay open in the host's world ([[Terraforming]] Q-55).

**Recommendation:** adopt the two-keys rule and the table above. Teach each move once in the Hollow before any region needs it.
