# Day Night and Seasons

**Purpose:** a clock and calendar that change what's possible, so *when* you go matters as much as where ([[Design Pillars]] 3, preparation is power).
**Inputs:** play time.
**Outputs:** time of day, moon phase, in-game season; modifiers on schools, stations and regions.

This note owns the **calendar**. Seasonal contests are [[Seasonal Contest Loop]].

## Two calendars
| Calendar | Runs on | Who shares it | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In-game seasons** | play time (7 play-days each) | personal, per world | everything on this page: growth, rivers, ice, rot speed |
| **Contest seasons** | real-world time, one per quarter | everyone | only the [[Community Layer]] and [[Seasonal Contest Loop]]; each ends with one real-world **Fair week**, the Wick Fair |

Missing a contest season costs nothing you already have. It is the only exception to the no-real-world-clocks rule in [[Anti-Chore Rules]].

## The clock
| Unit | Length (play time) | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Day | 20 min | dawn 2, day 10, dusk 2, night 6 |
| Moon cycle | 8 days | 8 phases, one per day (a 3-bit count: new = `000`, full = `100`) |
| Season | 7 days (~2 h 20 min) | Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter |
| Year | 28 days (~9 h 20 min) | 4 seasons |

- The clock runs only while playing. It pauses in menus, the cloak, the grimoire and story scenes.
- Sleep at your sanctum or a waystone to skip to the next dawn or dusk. Waiting is never required.

## What changes
| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Night** | the Observatory produces ([[Sanctum Stations]]); the Glasshigh's drifted star-paths mislead after dark; Star Roads only exist under their constellation; Star-step works |
| **Full moon** | Astral **full-moon bonus**: constellation bonus +0.25; Moonsilver yield; the Mirror Tarn becomes walkable ([[Ring Regions]]) |
| **New moon** | Barrowfen voices are easiest to coax back; Astral weakest |
| **Spring** | Bloom growth ×1.5; Garden ×1.5; rivers high (some fords closed, ferries faster) |
| **Summer** | Smith heat builds 1 step faster; Ironreach storms more frequent; Saltings vents more active; long days (day 12, night 4) |
| **Autumn** | **autumn rains**: rivers rise again, bog spreads, peat fires go out; Rot speed ×1.5 (Bloom freshness falls faster in the cloak); the grey blight advances faster; Ossuary input plentiful |
| **Winter** | Bloom dormant, Garden ×0.5 (never 0); water tiles 2+ freeze walkable; ferries close, ice routes open; long nights (day 8, night 8) |

## Seasons and contests
The in-game season flavors what a contest feels like in your world (Spring's high rivers suit a river contest), but contest timing comes from the real-world contest calendar only. The [[Seasonal Contest Loop]] decides what players do; this note decides nothing about contests.

## Limits and anti-chore rules
- Nothing important happens only once a year. Every seasonal resource or route returns next in-game season (about 9 hours of play).
- Season effects are bonuses and alternate routes, never sole keys to story progress.

**Q-54 (proposed answer):** in-game seasons are personal and run on play time; contest seasons are shared, real-world and quarterly, each closing with a real-world Fair week. Awaiting the designer.

**Recommendation:** 20-minute days and 7-day in-game seasons. An expedition of 30-60 minutes then crosses 1-3 days and sees at least one night.

## Scope cost
S-M. Lighting by time of day suits the low-res lit look ([[Art Direction]]); seasons need a palette swap per biome plus winter ice and autumn rain.
