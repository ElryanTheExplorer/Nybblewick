# Story Scene Loop

Play an authored scene: read the room, answer people with spells, live with the branch. You do it to find out what happens and to change who trusts you.

## Goal
Move your storyline forward, open a new place or verb, and see the branch you chose (and the ones you didn't) on the [[Branch Flowchart]].

## Steps
1. **Arrive:** a scene triggers at a place on an expedition, or from your order's seat.
2. **Prepare (optional):** the scene shows its theme before it starts (e.g. "a dispute at the ferry"). You can swap your loadout at a waystone first ([[Loadout Presets]]).
3. **Read:** look before acting ([[Astral Projection]], [[Familiar Vision]], Necro *remember*).
4. **Choose and cast:** dialogue options and casts as dialogue ([[Story Scene Structure]]).
5. **Beat:** 1-2 [[Chord QTEs]] at key moments, generous windows.
6. **Consequence:** flags set; Standing, world state and relationships change.
7. **Leaf:** at chapter end, the flowchart leaf fills in ([[Branch Flowchart]]).

## Where chording happens
- 2-6 casts per scene, each a verb chosen from your grimoire.
- QTE beats ask for one known address, timed to the player's measured fluency ([[Light-Up Drill]] data), never twitch-required (D-20). Misses branch, they don't fail.

## Rewards
- **Story:** what happens next; new characters.
- **Knowledge:** Voice spells at chapter ends (D-08), rune lessons.
- **Standing** with orders and towns ([[Standing]]).
- **World state:** a lit square, a moved road.
- **Looks:** the flowchart leaf itself; order regalia at tier changes.
See [[Incentive Model]].

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by [[Expedition Loop]] (scenes sit along routes) and [[Commission Loop]] (some commissions escalate into scenes).
- Feeds [[Restoration Loop]] (choices change regions), [[Research Loop]] (new verbs), Standing.

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: the default.
- Co-op: the host decides; guests' casts add options and they cast at QTE beats ([[Drop-In Co-op]], [[Cross-School Tensions]]).

## Pillars served
- Casting is speaking; Story through schools; The land answers ([[Design Pillars]]).

## Scope cost
**L.** Authored branches, cinematics and voice lines per school per premise. Mitigate: scenes reuse the isometric view with a few cinematic shots ([[Cinematic Scenes]]); branches rejoin within chapters.
