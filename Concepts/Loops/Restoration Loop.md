# Restoration Loop

Heal a region wound by wound until the land, its people and its order come back: the game's visible long-term arc ([[Loop Stack]]).

## Goal
Turn each of the six [[Ring Regions]] from its mood (the Drift, the Sour, the Grey Blight, the Silence, the Overcharge, the Severing) to restored, and see the Ring and the Hollow change.

## Steps
1. **Find a wound**: a restoration site, found while exploring or from a commission. Each region has 10-14 wounds plus one **heart** (its ritual).
2. **Resolve it**: an encounter ([[Encounter Types]]) or terraforming work ([[Terraforming]]).
3. **The land answers**: the site changes at once (lamp keeping time, spring clear, bridge whole, a dead voice answering) and stays changed.
4. **Stage up** when enough wounds are healed.
5. **Heart ritual** once stage 3 is reached; completing it gives stage 4.

## Stages
| Stage | Name | Needs | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Blighted | — | mood at full strength |
| 1 | Stirring | 3 wounds | first villagers return; better component quality nearby |
| 2 | Mending | 6 wounds | an order member settles in Nybblewick ([[Home Valley]]); ley line and Waystation parcels from this region |
| 3 | Thriving | 9 wounds | the heart ritual; station tier 3 for this school ([[Sanctum Stations]]); story chapter ([[Story and RPG MOC]]) |
| 4 | Restored | heart ritual | fixed Waydoor to the order's seat; the mood ends here; region palette brightens |

## Where chording happens
In every wound: 3-15 casts each, 15-40 for the heart. The wound's type says which verbs fit; at least two schools can solve any wound.

## Rewards
World state first (the land answers), then Standing with the region's order, new routes, better components, story.

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by: [[Expedition Loop]] (where wounds are healed), [[Research Loop]] (new verbs for new wounds), [[Ritual Raid Loop]] (hearts with friends).
- Feeds: [[Sanctum Loop]] (tier 3 gates), [[Transport Network]] (ley lines, Waydoors), [[Standing]], [[Story Scene Loop]].

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: every wound and heart is solvable alone.
- Co-op: guests' healing counts in the host's world; hearts have an optional co-op-only best outcome ([[Encounter Types]]).

## Pillars served
- The land answers (the main one); story through schools; together, mostly.

## Scope cost
L. About 70 wounds and 6 hearts across the Ring. Reuse wound templates (blight patch, dead lamp, broken bridge) with local dressing.
