# Duel Loop

Challenge, prepare a duel board, exchange calls and responses, settle the wager: a rare, consensual test of reading and preparation. Rules in [[Formal Duels]].

## Goal
Win a wager, settle a dispute, prove fluency to a rival, or call a thief to account ([[PvP Philosophy]]).

## Steps
1. Receive or send a challenge glyph with terms (tempo, wager, witnesses).
2. Pack a duel loadout: ward placement, bait cells, a saved Glamour ([[Loadout Presets]]), then declare the spell list ([[Formal Duels]] visibility rule).
3. Enter the circle; the view moves into the coat ([[In-the-Coat Duel View]]).
4. Exchange rounds of call and response until three Marks or a yield.
5. Settle the wager; witnesses record it; the loser may request one rematch.
6. Review: the **duel leaf** in the grimoire shows each round's chords, so you can study the opponent's tells.

## Where chording happens
- One chord pair per call, one per response. Six to twelve casts per duel.
- Speed is chosen in the challenge (Slow 10 s, Measured 6 s, Quick 3 s). Never forced (D-20).
- The skill is reading the other hand and arranging the board in advance.

## Rewards
- **Mastery** (reading, tempo), **Social** (witnessed result, Standing for honourable duels), wagered components with duel [[Provenance]]. See [[Incentive Model]].

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by: [[Subterfuge Loop]] (calling to account), [[Story Scene Loop]] (NPC rivals), [[Drop-In Co-op]] disputes.
- Feeds: [[Notoriety and Oaths]] (Oath of the Witness), [[Gift and Provenance Economy]] (trophy components).

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: NPC story duels against order rivals teach the form.
- Co-op: friend duels in a shared session; co-op guests can witness.

## Pillars served
- Casting is speaking (reading hands), Preparation is power (the duel board), Together mostly (consensual only).

## Scope cost
M: needs the first-person view, a small spell set (a ward, Unweave, Glamour, Tether), and turn sync. No new world content.
