# Expedition Loop

Leave the Hollow with a packed cloak, cross a region, come back changed: the session spine (30-60 min) from the [[Loop Stack]].

## Goal
Components with good quality and provenance, a healed wound or two, a story beat, Standing. Then home to unpack and plan the next trip.

## Steps
1. **Prepare** (5 min, Hollow): pick a destination from the region map, a commission or a story lead; check the day, moon and season ([[Day Night and Seasons]]); pack the cloak and choose a [[Loadout Presets|preset]].
2. **Travel** (2-10 min): road, ferry, tether, waystone or Waydoor ([[Transport Network]]). Short on purpose.
3. **Scout** (1-3 min): read the area by [[Astral Projection]] or [[Familiar Vision]] ([[Astral Scouting Loop]]). Mark components, gates and encounters.
4. **Forage** (throughout): gather components; borderlands for duals ([[Ring Regions]]).
5. **Encounter** (2-4 encounters, 1-15 min each): hazards, fronts, spirits, broken things, a ritual if ready ([[Encounter Types]]). Hazards build Strain; full Strain means you faint and wake at the last waystone with everything you carried.
6. **Rest** (optional): wake a waystone, swap loadout, open a shortcut.
7. **Return** (1-5 min): walk the shortcuts, take a Waydoor, or rest at a waystone and travel home.
8. **Unpack** (3-5 min): harvest stations ([[Sanctum Loop]]), deliver commissions, start research ([[Research Loop]]).

## Where chording happens
- Throughout: 30-80 casts a session, mostly utility (light, grow, mend, ferry).
- Encounters and rituals bunch casts into short runs. Speed is never required (D-20).
- Preparing the cloak is the non-chord half: the board puzzle ([[Polyomino Spells]]).

## Rewards
Capability (components, new routes), story (scenes, lore), Standing, world state ([[Incentive Model]]). No currency.

## Feeds into / fed by
- Feeds: [[Restoration Loop]] (healed wounds), [[Sanctum Loop]] (inputs, upgrade costs), [[Research Loop]] (finds), [[Commission Loop]].
- Fed by: Sanctum (supplies, Spark), Research (new spells), Restoration (shortcuts, Waydoors), [[Story Scene Loop]] (leads).

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: the default. Bound spells and longer windows cover multi-step jobs.
- Co-op: guests drop into the host's expedition ([[Drop-In Co-op]]); each keeps what they forage; roles split at encounters.

## Pillars served
- The land answers; preparation is power; casting is speaking; together, mostly ([[Design Pillars]]).

## Scope cost
L. It needs a region, encounters, foraging, travel and return. But it is the game; every other loop hangs off it. Slice version: one region edge, 3 encounters, 10 component nodes.
