# Commission Loop

Townsfolk ask for help: light the square, mend the bridge, ferry the cart. You plan the job, pack for it, do it with your verbs, and the town remembers. This is **Loop Candidate 1** from [[Gameplay Loop Candidates]] ("apprentice's lessons → errands"), grown up: the lessons became the [[Light-Up Drill]] onboarding, and the errands became commissions that use the whole kit.

## Goal
Help a specific person with a specific problem, earn Standing with their town or order, and leave a visible change.

## Steps
1. **Read the board:** each town has a commission board (a pinned notice, a lamp-post with ribbons). Each commission names a person, a place and a problem, never a reward number.
2. **Plan:** which verbs solve it? Some commissions have more than one solution (mend the bridge with Bloom, or forge a new one with Smith).
3. **Forage and pack:** fetch what the job needs; arrange the cloak ([[Cloak Inventory]]).
4. **Do the job:** travel, cast, solve. Usually 1-5 minutes ([[Encounter Types]]).
5. **Return or not:** the result is visible at once; you only go back to hear thanks if you want to.
6. **Ripple:** some commissions escalate into a [[Story Scene Loop|story scene]] or a chapter of a storyline.

## Example commissions
| Who | Problem | Verbs that solve it |
|---|---|---|
| A baker in Nybblewick | the square's lamps are out before the night market | Astral light; Smith power |
| A Saltings widow | her husband's last letter is crumbling | Alchemy preserve; Necro remember |
| Two farmers | a fence is broken and they blame each other | Bloom mend; Smith repair; Wayfarer tether the goats |
| A ferry child | the rope keeps snapping | Wayfarer tether; Smith forge |

## Where chording happens
3-10 casts per commission, at the player's own pace. No timers on commissions, ever (D-20).

## Rewards
- **Standing** with the town and the asker's order ([[Standing]]).
- **World state:** the lamp stays lit, the bridge stays mended.
- **Gifts:** components with local [[Provenance]] ([[Gift and Provenance Economy]]).
- Occasionally **Knowledge**: a townsperson teaches a folk variant of a spell.
No currency (D-28).

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by: [[Restoration Loop]] (restored places produce new requests), storylines (side effects of choices).
- Feeds: [[Standing]], [[Story Scene Loop]], [[Sanctum Loop]] (gifts), fluency practice.

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: the default; a calm filler between chapters.
- Co-op: a guest can take a commission in the host's world; both earn Standing there.

## Pillars served
- The land answers; Casting is speaking; Preparation is power ([[Design Pillars]]).

## Scope cost
**M.** Templates (person + place + problem + verb set) are cheap; the cost is world reactions per verb. Anti-chore: commissions never expire and the board never nags ([[Anti-Chore Rules]]).

**Q-47:** authored commissions, generated ones, or both? Lean both: authored ones carry names and story; generated ones (from a person + problem + verb template) fill the board, capped so the board never shows more than 6 (matching [[Anti-Chore Rules]]).
