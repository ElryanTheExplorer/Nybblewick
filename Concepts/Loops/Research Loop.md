# Research Loop

Hear of a spell, find its pieces, try it until the land answers, and write it into your grimoire. New spells are new words ([[Spellbook as Vocabulary]]).

## Goal
A new verb, a new recipe, or a new catalyst variant in the grimoire: more ways to change the world.

## Steps
1. **Lead.** A rumor of a spell, from one of:
   - a **Voice** storyline: a mentor teaches it in a scene (D-08, [[Story Scene Loop]]);
   - **ruins**: a half-legible inscription gives the footprint and some sockets ([[Rune Puzzles]], [[Datasheet Grimoires]]);
   - a friend's **loadout code** with an unknown ID ([[Loadout Codes]]);
   - an **Omen** or **Echo** cast that shows someone using it ([[Astral Spellbook]], [[Necro Spellbook]]).
2. **Fragment.** The lead becomes a grimoire entry with blanks: footprint known, sockets shown as `?`, verb named. Example: "Wake the forge: `O4`, `{rot}{?}{any}{any}`".
3. **Experiment.** At the sanctum's lectern (or any waystone), lay the fragment on a scratch leaf and try components around it. Each test cast reports which sockets took a tag and which stayed empty, like [[XOR Feedback]] for recipes. No component is spent on a scratch leaf, whatever Q-01 decides.
4. **Field test.** The first full-power cast must be at a real target (a cold forge, a dark lamp). The land answers, the spell is proven.
5. **Inscribe.** Write it into the grimoire. Choose its cell on a page, or leave it in the book until you have pockets ([[Cloak Pocket Progression]]).
6. **Extend (optional).** Keep experimenting with the proven spell to find **alt recipes** and **catalyst variants** ([[Catalysts]]).

## Where chording happens
- Test casts on the scratch leaf: several casts of the same address, slow and calm. A fluency exercise on cells you'll use for real.
- The field test: one cast, no time pressure. Never twitch-required ([[Not Doing]]).

## Rewards
- **Knowledge** (the spell, alt recipes, catalyst variants): the main reward ([[Incentive Model]]).
- **Standing** with the teaching order when a Voice-taught spell is first used in its story ([[Standing]]).
- **World state** from the field test target.
- Some catalysts appear only by experiment ([[Component Catalog]]).

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by [[Expedition Loop]] (ruins, components), [[Story Scene Loop]] (Voice teaching), [[Commission Loop]] ("we need a spell that…"), [[Drop-In Co-op]] (codes).
- Feeds [[Expedition Loop]] (new verbs open gated routes, [[Traversal and Verb Gates]]), [[Restoration Loop]], [[Loadout Presets]].

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: the full loop.
- Co-op: two players each hold half a fragment (each saw a different inscription); they combine at one lectern. Both learn it.

## Pillars served
- Casting is speaking (new words), Preparation is power (recipes found before they're needed), The land answers (the field test).

## Scope cost
**M.** The lectern UI and socket feedback reuse the board. Each spell needs a lead source and a field-test target, which are authoring work but small per spell.

## Experiment vs. teaching (Q-37)
**Q-37:** Can a whole new spell be discovered by experimentation alone, or does every spell need a lead?
- Experiment-only: Little Alchemy-style freedom (Plate IV, [[Inventory Plates]]); risks guessing games.
- Lead-only: every spell has a story; less player-driven discovery.

**Recommendation:** new **spells** need a lead (story, ruin, code, omen); new **recipes and catalyst variants** of known spells are discovered by experiment. Spells stay story-bound (D-08 Voice), while tinkering stays rewarding.
