# Astral Scouting Loop

Leave your body, look ahead, mark what matters, snap back and act on it. A phase inside the [[Expedition Loop]] that turns "read" in read → prepare → resolve into play.

## Goal
Know an encounter before committing your cloak to it: what's there, which verbs it needs, where the rare components grow.

## Steps
1. **Find a safe spot.** Sheltered ground lowers ambient risk to the body ([[Astral Projection]]).
2. **Project out.** Cast *Project* ([[Astral Spellbook]]); the camera detaches into astral view ([[Context Camera Modes]]).
3. **Scout.** Drift up to the tether limit; see through fog and thin walls; read telegraphs, spirits and provenance trails.
4. **Mark.** Pin up to 5 marks: a target, a hazard, a component patch, a seal to trace, a route.
5. **Return.** Recall at will, or snap back when the breath runs out. A body ping calls you back early if something nears your body.
6. **Prepare.** Open the cloak and rearrange components for what you saw ([[Loadout Presets]]).
7. **Act.** Walk in and resolve the encounter with the marks still visible.

## Where chording happens
- One cast of *Project*; 0-5 marks (each a short projected spell); one optional lure or whisper.
- None needs speed. The breath timer limits how long you look, not how fast you chord.

## Rewards
- Information, which turns into better outcomes (fewer wasted components, safer routes).
- Discovery: rare component patches and hidden landmarks, which feed [[Provenance]] and [[Sanctum Decor]] trophies.
- Story hooks: spirits you whisper to can start [[Commission Loop]] errands.

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by: [[Expedition Loop]] (a new area, an unknown encounter), astral components (Starglass, Moonsilver), and the night sky: the full-moon bonus doubles the tether ([[Day Night and Seasons]]).
- Feeds: [[Encounter Types]] (resolved better), [[Loadout Presets]] (re-packing), [[Restoration Loop]] (spotting blight before it spreads).

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: you balance tether time against body risk.
- Co-op: one wizard projects while another guards the body; marks are shared. A familiar owner can scout physically at the same time ([[Familiar Vision]]), so two views map a place from both layers.

## Pillars served
- 3 Preparation is power (the best moment is before you act).
- 4 Story through schools (Astral sees what others can't).
- 5 Together, mostly (guarding and shared marks).
