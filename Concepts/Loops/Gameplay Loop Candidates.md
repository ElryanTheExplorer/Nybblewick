# Gameplay Loop Candidates

> **Pass 2 (2026-11-14):** these candidates grew into full loop notes, arranged in the [[Loop Stack]] (see [[Loops MOC]]). 1 → [[Commission Loop]] + [[Light-Up Drill]] lessons; 2 → [[Expedition Loop]]; 3 → [[Story Scene Loop]]; 4 → [[Rune Puzzles]] + [[2.5D Segments]]; 5 → [[Ritual Raid Loop]]; 6 → [[Sanctum Loop]] + [[Restoration Loop]].

**Q-16:** what's the core loop? The designer is still deciding. These are **Proposals** to argue with.

The test for each: does it make chording feel good, and does it give a reason to get fluent?

## 1. Apprentice's lessons → errands
Daily lessons ([[Light-Up Drill]]) unlock spells; townsfolk post utility jobs (light the square, mend the bridge, ferry the cart); you forage, arrange the cloak, perform. Strong teaching, cozy pace.

## 2. Forage → arrange → cast
Expeditions into biomes ([[The Land's Moods]]) gather [[Components]]; back home you pack the [[Cloak Inventory]]; the next trip tests the build. The board puzzle is the core.

## 3. Spells as sentences
Story scenes ask questions the world answers to verbs. Your chord vocabulary and the components you carry decide branches ([[Spellbook as Vocabulary]]). Choose-your-own-adventure.

## 4. Seal-breaking rooms
Each location is a rune puzzle ([[Rune Puzzles]]): gates, parity, Gray code. Chords are the solution input. Zachtronics-style.

## 5. Ritual rhythm
Big casts are timed chord sequences, solo or in a choir ([[Choir Casting]]). Rhythm-game feel; speed matters but is musical, not reactive.

## 6. Town tending
Your spells shape a settlement over seasons (Bloom fields, Smith forges, Wayfarer roads). Communities contest seasons and rivers ([[Community Ritual Conflict]]).

## Notes
- These stack: 1 is onboarding, 2 or 6 the spine, 3 and 4 the content, 5 the endgame.
- A tier-0 prototype of loop 1 is the cheapest real test ([[Scope Tiers]]).
