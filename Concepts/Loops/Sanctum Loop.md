# Sanctum Loop

Build, upgrade and harvest stations in the Hollow so each expedition starts better supplied than the last: the long-term home loop ([[Loop Stack]]).

## Goal
A working sanctum: six stations, linked by tethers and bound spells, that grows basic components and Spark, and shows how far you've come.

## Steps
1. **Build** a station on your plot (school components + notes) ([[Sanctum Stations]]).
2. **Supply** it: tether water, seed stock or Spark ([[Automation]]).
3. **Inscribe** bound spells you've already cast by hand.
4. **Leave** on an expedition; stations run during play.
5. **Harvest** on return: one walk-by of the store chest.
6. **Upgrade** when you have the components, Standing (Trusted for tier 2, Kin for tier 3) and restoration stage.
7. **Rearrange** tethers and spells to fix Spark shortfalls (the throughput puzzle).

## Where chording happens
- Inscribing bound spells: 1 cast each, done once.
- Building and upgrading: a short build cast per station (3-5 chords).
- Terraforming the plot ([[Terraforming]]) as much or as little as you like.
- Light on chords by design: the Sanctum is the rest between expeditions.

## Rewards
Capability (a steady basic supply, Spark), world state (a growing valley), gifts with Hollow provenance ([[Gift and Provenance Economy]]), looks ([[Sanctum Decor]]).

## Feeds into / fed by
- Feeds: [[Expedition Loop]] (supplies), [[Automation]], [[Commission Loop]] (deliveries).
- Fed by: Expedition (inputs, upgrade costs), [[Restoration Loop]] (tier 3 gates, Waystation parcels), [[Standing]] (Trusted and Kin gates), [[Research Loop]] (notes).

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: the default; the Hollow is yours.
- Co-op: guests can visit, help build and see your network; the Hollow is a natural hosting lobby ([[Drop-In Co-op]]).

## Pillars served
- The land answers; preparation is power.

## Anti-chore check
Stations run on play time only, never spoil stored items, and never need daily visits ([[Anti-Chore Rules]]). Skipping the Sanctum for a week costs nothing but convenience.

## Scope cost
M. Six stations, one rate system, tethers. Looks are the larger cost and sit with [[Sanctum Decor]].
