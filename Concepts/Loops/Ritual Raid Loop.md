# Ritual Raid Loop

Gather up to four wizards to perform a great ritual against a world threat. The "boss" is a ritual with phases and seats, not a health bar ([[Encounter Types]]). This is the social peak of the [[Loop Stack]].

## Goal
Resolve a threat that changes a region: calm a blight tide, re-moor fallen stars, lay a drowned ferry-spirit to rest, bank a storm.

## Steps
1. **Omen:** a threat appears in the host's world (a blight front spreading from the Mulch, a storm stalled over the Stormline).
2. **Read it:** Astral scouting or familiars reveal its phases and which verbs it answers to ([[Astral Scouting Loop]]).
3. **Seat the circle:** assign seats: **Anchor** (holds a ward), **Caller** (casts the call sequence), **Weaver** (moves components between boards, Wayfarer), **Reader** (calls the threat's next telegraph, Astral).
4. **Gather offerings:** the ritual names the components it wants; the party brings them ([[Component Catalog]]).
5. **Perform:** each phase is a [[Choir Casting]] sequence plus Shared Bytes ([[Drop-In Co-op]]). A missed part slips the ritual back one phase; nothing wipes.
6. **Aftermath:** the region changes for good (blight becomes rich Rot fields; the storm leaves struck lodestone on the ridge). Everyone takes Keepsakes.

## Example: the Blight Tide (Bloom + Necro)
- Phase 1, Contain: Anchor wards the field edge; Caller grows a Briar hedge line.
- Phase 2, Turn: a Necro player raises the rot so it stops spreading, Bloom players mend behind it.
- Phase 3, Settle: a four-part choir chord seeds the field. The Mulch gains a new ghostcap grove.

## Where chording happens
- 10 to 30 casts per player over 15 to 30 minutes. Choir windows are at least 4 s per part; a **Tight** window is an optional flourish for groups that want it. Each part resolves like a [[Chord QTEs|chord QTE]]: clean, late or wrong, each with its own result (late holds the phase, wrong slips it back).
- Hard parts are coordination, not speed. Solo players get a slower tempo.

## Rewards
- **Visible world change** (the region transforms), **Social** (Keepsakes, Standing with affected orders), **Capability** (a rare component or rune per threat). See [[Incentive Model]].

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by: [[Expedition Loop]] (finding omens), [[Restoration Loop]] (threats block restoration).
- Feeds: [[Restoration Loop]], [[Community Layer]] (shared seasonal goals count raids), [[Gift and Provenance Economy]].

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: familiars and order NPCs fill empty seats; the threat is smaller but the same shape.
- Co-op: 2 to 4 players; each extra player opens an optional phase with a better aftermath.

## Pillars served
- The land answers, Preparation is power, Together mostly.

## Scope cost
L: each threat is authored content (phases, seats, aftermath art). Start with one per region pair.
