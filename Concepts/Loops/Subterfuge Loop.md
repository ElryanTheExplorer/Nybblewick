# Subterfuge Loop

Spot an opportunity, prepare a deception, act, and live with the trace. The sanctioned way to be sneaky, inside sessions and contests that allow it ([[Subterfuge]]).

## Goal
Win a scarce rare, swing a contest, learn another order's secret, or complete a heist commission.

## Steps
1. **Opportunity:** a meteor fall in a shared session, a rival town's stockpile, a heist commission ([[Commission Loop]]), another order's scene.
2. **Check the rules:** the session's Hearth Rule or the contest's terms ([[PvP Philosophy]]).
3. **Scout:** Astral projection or a familiar finds wards and watchmen ([[Astral Scouting Loop]]).
4. **Prepare:** pack the right spells: Glamour (a disguise needs its catalyst variant), Lodepull, a Glamour decoy ([[Astral Spellbook]], [[Wayfarer Spellbook]]).
5. **Act:** usually two to four casts at chosen moments.
6. **Trace:** stolen goods carry provenance; residue lingers for divination; a caught act adds Notoriety or draws a challenge.
7. **Resolve:** keep it, return it for redemption, or answer the challenge ([[Duel Loop]]).

## Where chording happens
- Few casts, chosen moments. Timing is about *when*, not how fast.
- Defenders chord wards and divinations ahead of time (preparation beats reaction).

## Rewards
- **Discovery** (secrets), **Social** (contest swing, reputation), occasional rare components. See [[Incentive Model]].
- Cost side: [[Notoriety and Oaths]], provenance stains.

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by: [[Seasonal Contest Loop]], [[Drop-In Co-op]] sessions, NPC heist commissions.
- Feeds: [[Duel Loop]], Notoriety, the rogue path of back-road barterers ([[Notoriety and Oaths]]).

## Solo / co-op
- Solo: NPC heist commissions and disguised scene infiltration give the whole loop without other players.
- Co-op: Mischief (pranks) and Cutpurse sessions; contests.

## Pillars served
- Preparation is power, Story through schools (Astral and Wayfarer shine). Must not break Together mostly: costs are always visible.

## Scope cost
M: reuses verbs and provenance; needs residue, Hearth Rules and Notoriety UI.
