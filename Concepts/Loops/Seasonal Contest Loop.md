# Seasonal Contest Loop

Over one **contest season**, towns compete asynchronously for something the land offers: a river's flow, the harvest, the long night's lamps. Players pledge, contribute from their own worlds, and gather for a closing rite at the Wick Fair. Fiction in [[Community Ritual Conflict]].

**Two calendars** ([[Canon Sheet]]): *in-game seasons* are personal and run on play time ([[Day Night and Seasons]]). *Contest seasons* are shared and real-world, one per quarter, and exist only for this loop and the [[Community Layer]]. Missing a contest season costs nothing you already have; this is the single exception to [[Anti-Chore Rules]] rule 7.

## Goal
Help your pledged town win the contest season's prize: a festival and a cosmetic or communal mark in the world. Prizes never grant power or story.

## Example contests (one per contest season)
| Season | Contest | Contributed by | Prize for the winning town |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring quarter | **The River Rite**: whose fields does the thaw river feed? | Ferry and channel spells, Bloom planting | The river bends toward their region's art next season |
| Summer quarter | **The Long Lamps**: whose road burns brightest? | Lamps lit, Astral light shows | A lantern festival in that town |
| Autumn quarter | **The Harvest Weighing** | Offerings of components, judged by quality and provenance | The harvest fair is held in their town: a seed-swap open to every player, and harvest garlands on their roofs |
| Winter quarter | **The Barrow Vigil**: who keeps the dead's stories? | Necro remembrances, Smith hearths | Vigil lanterns along their barrow road. The **ancestors' tales** told at the Vigil are lore every town gets, win or lose (rule 9) |

## Steps
1. **Pledge** to a town (default: your order's seat).
2. **Contribute** through normal play in your own world: each relevant deed adds to the town tally.
3. **Gambits:** contest-only moves, including [[Subterfuge]] such as a Blight Gambit on a rival's Bloom offering, or a Wayfarer redirect of a rival's river channel.
4. **Closing rite at the Wick Fair:** one real-world Fair week at the end of each contest season. Every pledged player can add one choir part from home, at any time that week. Wickwax drops at the Fair.
5. **Result:** the winner's prize changes the world; everyone pledged gets a seasonal keepsake.

## Where chording happens
- Mostly the casts you already make (lighting, growing, ferrying). The closing rite adds one choir sequence.
- No speed demands; contribution is volume over the contest season, not reflex.

## Rewards
- **Social** (town result, keepsakes), **Visible world change** (prize changes the world), **Expression** (seasonal cosmetics). See [[Incentive Model]].

## Feeds into / fed by
- Fed by: [[Restoration Loop]], [[Expedition Loop]], [[Sanctum Loop]] output, [[Gift and Provenance Economy]] (offerings).
- Feeds: [[Community Layer]] seasonal goals, [[Subterfuge Loop]], [[Standing]] with towns.

## Solo / co-op
- Solo players contribute fully from their own world. Co-op raids during the season count double for the host's and guests' pledged towns.

## Pillars served
- Together mostly, The land answers. Must obey [[Anti-Chore Rules]]: late joiners get a catch-up multiplier, prizes return in later years, no story is locked to a contest.

## Scope cost
M to L: server tallies, a seasonal results screen, four prize variants per town.
