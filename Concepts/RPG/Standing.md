# Standing

Your relationship with each **order** and each **town**. It gates story and services and does the work currency would do in another RPG. There is no gold ([[Canon Sheet]], D-28).

## Tiers (Proposal)
| Tier | Meaning | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Stranger | they don't know you | public places, commissions board |
| Known | you've helped once or twice | apprenticeship scenes, basic gifts of components |
| Trusted | you've helped with something hard | storyline Act II, station tier 2, passage on the order's opened routes, lodging, tutors |
| Kin | they count you as one of theirs | Act III order rites, rare components, station tier 3 (with region stage 3) |
| Sworn | optional, via the **Oath of the Order** ([[Notoriety and Oaths]]) | an order's deepest secret; a boon |

Shown as **words and signs** (a banner in your sanctum, how NPCs greet you), not a number.

## Earned by
- Finishing commissions for them ([[Commission Loop]]).
- Restoring their region ([[Restoration Loop]]).
- Story choices at flashpoints ([[Cross-School Tensions]]).
- Gifts whose [[Provenance]] means something to them (Glasshigh starglass to the Starwatch, not Saltings salt).
- Co-op rituals in their region ([[Ritual Raid Loop]]).
- Teaching: helping a newer player or NPC apprentice.

## Lost by
- Authored choices against them (the only way it's zero-sum).
- Harming their land (a burned firebreak costs Hedge Circle Standing).
- Breaking an Oath or being caught in subterfuge (rules in [[Notoriety and Oaths]]).
- **Never** by absence or time ([[Anti-Chore Rules]]).

## How it replaces currency
| Instead of buying... | You... |
|---|---|
| components | are given them by orders you're Known to |
| spells | are taught them in Voice storylines at Trusted |
| travel | are let through the order's Waydoors and ferries at Trusted, only on routes restoration has opened ([[Transport Network]]) |
| station upgrades | unlock tier 2 at Trusted, tier 3 at Kin plus region stage 3 ([[Sanctum Stations]]) |
| looks | are given order regalia at each tier ([[Wizard Drip]]) |

Standing is not spent. Getting something doesn't lower it.

## Towns
Nybblewick and each region's settlements have their own Standing, separate from orders. A town can like you while its order doesn't: that gap drives commissions and scenes.

**Q-44:** should Standing ever be visible as a number (even in a menu)? Options: (a) words and signs only; (b) a hidden number with visible tier; (c) a number in an accessibility or "ledger" view. Lean (b): designers need it, players see tiers.
