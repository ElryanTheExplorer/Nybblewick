# Horizontal Progression

You grow by being able to **do more things**, not by doing the same things with bigger numbers. An RPG (D-26) without levels, HP scaling or damage tiers.

## What grows
| Axis ([[Canon Sheet]]) | Grows by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Fluency | player practice | casting `C7` without looking ([[Fluency as Skill]]) |
| Grimoire (**Voice**, D-08) | storylines teach new verbs | Necro Act I teaches *speak with the dead* |
| Cloak | more board area and sockets | a 4-cell patch is sewn where you choose ([[Cloak Pocket Progression]]) |
| Familiar (**Pact**, D-08) | new senses | night sight, scent, echo; species is a look only |
| Runes (**Rune**, D-08) | new seal types you can read | [[Gray Code Locks]] open the Old Orrery |
| Components | new kinds, Storied provenance | Starsalt from the borderland unlocks a hybrid |
| Sanctum | new stations | a still-room grows salts while you travel ([[Sanctum Stations]]) |
| Standing | trust with orders and towns | the Waymarch lets you through its opened Waydoors ([[Standing]]) |
| Story | flags and world state | the restored road is a new route |

## Spells grow sideways
- A spell gains **variants**, not ranks: same verb, different shape, sockets or target ([[Polyomino Spells]], [[Sockets and Components]]).
- Example: *mend* first fixes a fence. Later variants mend a bridge (bigger shape, needs Moss mat) or mend at range (needs a Wayfarer socket). Each is a new thing to do, not "+20%".
- Hybrids are new verbs ([[Hybrid Spells]]).

## Where vertical scaling is allowed
Only where a number is the honest expression of the thing, and capped:
- **Component quality** (Plain, Fine, Storied, [[Component Taxonomy]]): never changes power. Fine adds a state point (a charge, freshness, heat headroom); Storied adds a provenance NPCs recognise and can be a story key.
- **Scale of target**: what size of thing a verb can affect (a lamp, a street, a valley). Expressed as new variants, so it stays horizontal on the board.
- **Station throughput**: numbers live in World/Sanctum notes ([[Sanctum Stations]]).
- **Fluency**: faster casting is a real skill gain, optional (D-20).

Never: player level, health pools, damage stats, gear score.

**Q-45:** is "scale of target" a variant (a new spell on the board) or a property a single spell grows into (e.g. through a catalyst, [[Catalysts]])? Lean variant: it keeps board space meaningful and fits D-04.

## Why
- Fluency is the level ([[Design Pillars]] 1). Numbers would compete with it.
- Utility verbs are binary in story (the bridge is mended or not), so gates are verbs, not stats ([[Traversal and Verb Gates]]).
- Co-op works across progress gaps: a new player brings verbs, not low numbers ([[Drop-In Co-op]]).
