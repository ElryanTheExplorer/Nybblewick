# Origins and Splashing

You start as an apprentice of one order (your **origin**). Later you can **splash** a second school. Staying mono-school earns [[Devotion]]. Baldur's Gate 3 origins as mechanic reference ([[Reference Deconstruction]]).

## Choosing an origin
Chosen in the prologue, in Nybblewick, by which mentor's lesson you finish first at the Wick (a scene, not a menu; a menu is offered for replays).

What it changes:
- **Storyline:** your order's chapter is the main thread ([[Astral Storyline]] etc.).
- **First verbs:** one or two Voice spells from that school (D-08).
- **Starting Standing:** Known with your order (Trusted opens its Act II), Stranger with everyone else ([[Standing]]).
- **Starting cloak:** a few components of your school's region, placed by your mentor. Schools never own board rows (D-04).
- **Stance and look:** your hand-pose variant ([[School Stances]]) and order colors ([[Wizard Drip]]).
- **First region:** your order's seat is your first expedition.

Launch count is **Q-03**. D-05 names Astral, Alchemy, Wayfarer (locomotion) and Bloom (plants) as starting schools; Smith and Necro could be splash-only at first and become origins later.

## Splashing a second school
- **When:** after Act I of your origin storyline. Before that, the game is teaching one vocabulary.
- **How:** reach Known with the second order and finish its apprenticeship scene. Their storyline opens at Act I.
- **Neighbor splash** (easy): borderland duals are close; flashpoint scenes give you hybrid branches ([[Cross-School Tensions]]).
- **Checking-school splash** (harder): the order wants proof; an extra trial scene.
- **Opposite splash**: allowed, rare lore, few hybrids.
- Third schools: allowed but slow; each extra school dilutes Devotion.

## Devotion payoff
Horizontal, never a stat bonus ([[Horizontal Progression]]):
- **Order rites:** unique Act III branches only a devoted apprentice can take.
- **Signature variant:** one spell variant per school, available only while mono-school.
- **Deep component behavior:** your school's components behave one step further (e.g. Bloom freshness pauses while in the cloak). Exact rules belong to the Spells notes ([[Devotion]]).
- Devotion is measured by what's on your board, not by your past. Unsocket the splash and Devotion returns.

## Respec
- **Loadout:** free at any waystone or the sanctum ([[Loadout Presets]]).
- **Splash:** drop or swap freely; Standing with that order stays.
- **Origin:** permanent for the story (it's who you are), but an authored late-game scene, **the Turning**, lets you re-pledge to another order once per playthrough, at a Standing cost with the old one.

**Q-41:** should splashing be open from the start for experienced players (a "veteran" toggle), or always gated behind Act I? Lean gated, with a toggle on New Game+.
