# 2.5D Segments

Short side-view stretches where the camera locks to a plane and the game becomes a light platformer: the designer's "shift into 2D-ish". Same 3D scene, orthographic side camera on a spline ([[Context Camera Modes]]).

## When they happen
- **Cliffs and climbs:** the Ironreach storm ridges, a Glasshigh observatory tower's outer stair.
- **Cutaway interiors:** a dollhouse slice through a ruined tower or a barrow, rooms stacked.
- **Chases:** a fleeing spirit or a rolling flood. The chaser **waits** for you at each foothold; chases set mood, not speed checks (D-20).
- **Inside a seal:** you walk as a spark along the traces of a [[PCB Seals|PCB seal]] or a [[Logic Gate Runes|logic-gate rune]]; chords flip gates ahead of you. This is a puzzle that happens to be side-on.
- **Verb gates:** places that test a traversal spell from the side ([[Traversal and Verb Gates]]).

## How movement and chords share hands
- Movement and chords **take turns**. You chord on a **foothold**: a ledge, a rope, a lit rune. Standing still is always safe.
- Keyboard: left hand `A`/`D`/`W`, right hand chords on `7 8 9 0 b` (D-16 holds here because the mouse isn't used). Details per device: [[Input Budget per Mode]].
- Aiming follows the plane: Lodestep targets the next ledge in facing, Root bridge the nearest gap edge. No mouse.
- Mid-air casting exists only as optional mastery (a faster route), never as the only way through.

## Movement feel (minimum)
- Walk, jump, ledge-grab, climb rope. No double jump, no dash, no wall jump.
- Falling costs a few seconds (you reappear at the last foothold). No damage.

## Scope warning
Platforming is a craft of its own: jump feel, coyote time, camera lead, level rhythm. Every segment is hand-built and tested.
- **Recommendation:** build the seal-interior segment first; it's a puzzle, so it needs no jump tuning. Add cliffs and cutaways only if seal interiors land well, at most one per region. Chases last.
- Launch tier: last (Q-23, [[Decision Gates]]).
