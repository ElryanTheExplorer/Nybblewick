# Astral Projection

You leave your body and look around as a spirit, tethered to it by a thread of light. The designer's flavor for out-of-body viewing. It's a **sibling** of [[Familiar Vision]], not a replacement.

## How it differs from familiar vision
| | Astral projection | Familiar vision |
|---|---|---|
| Source | *Project*, an [[Astral]] spell in the [[Astral Spellbook]] (needs an astral component, e.g. Starglass) | A Pact familiar ([[Progression Axes]], D-08) |
| Body | Your spirit; your body stays behind, vulnerable | The familiar's body; yours stays in control |
| Sees | The spirit layer: telegraphs, spirits, provenance trails, seal wiring | The physical world from a low or high vantage |
| Moves | Through thin walls and fog, not through warded ground | Like the animal: a bird flies, a cat squeezes through gaps |
| Can act | Marks, lures, whispers (no physical effect) | Small physical acts if Q-10 allows (fetch, press a plate) |
| Seen by | Spirits and Astral NPCs only | Everyone; can be chased or fed |

**Recommendation for Q-10:** keep them distinct. The familiar is the **physical** scout (may act in small ways); projection is the **informational** scout (view and mark only). A player can own both and pick per situation.

## The body left behind
- Your body kneels where you cast, cloak drawn shut. You see it as a dim silhouette with a pulse.
- Risk is **ambient, never lethal**: the tide rises in the Saltings, a magpie pecks a component out of an open pocket, frost settles on Bloom components (freshness ticks), a wary spirit moves your body a few tiles.
- A **body ping** (the tether shivers, a heartbeat sound) warns you 5 s before anything happens. Returning is instant.
- Co-op: a partner can stand guard (risks don't fire while a friend is adjacent). Whether a non-friend can mess with your body is a [[Subterfuge]] question (Q-72).

## Range and time (starting numbers, to tune)
- **Tether:** 24 tiles at first; +8 with Moonsilver in the cloak; doubled at night under a full moon (Astral's **full-moon bonus**; moon phases follow [[Day Night and Seasons]]).
- **Breath:** 45 s per projection, shown as a thinning tether. No penalty when it runs out: a hard cut back to the body and a 2 s daze.
- **Warded ground** (iron, salt lines, Barrowfen barrows) stops the projection. That makes wards readable puzzles, not walls.

## What you can do while projected
- **Mark** a tile or object (a pin visible after you return, and to co-op partners).
- **Read** encounter telegraphs, spirit moods, where a component grows ([[Provenance]] trails).
- **Lure** a light-loving spirit or creature toward a mark.
- **Whisper** a short message to a spirit ([[Serial Spirits]]).
- **Trace** a seal's wiring from inside a wall before solving it ([[PCB Seals]]).
- Not allowed: picking up, opening, casting physical spells.

## Who can project
- *Project* is in the [[Astral Spellbook]]. Astral-school players learn it early; other schools can learn it as a splash ([[Origins and Splashing]]) with a shorter tether. Non-Astral players lean on familiars instead.
