# Orb Input Device

A wizard-themed orb with capacitive touch regions (D-18).

## Proposals
- Calibrate per user: rest the hand, record five contact centroids.
- Or force sensing, so finger count stays constant.
- An IMU inside: rotate the orb to aim.
- Two or three diameters, or a modular shell.
- Prototype first with drum pads or force pads.
