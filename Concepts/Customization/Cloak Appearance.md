# Cloak Appearance

How the cloak looks, outside and in. The cloak's **capacity and layout** (how many pockets, what shape) belong to [[Cloak Pocket Progression]]; this note covers only looks.

## Three layers
1. **Shell** (seen in iso and scenes): cut (short cape, long coat, hooded mantle), collar, clasp, hem.
2. **Lining** (seen when the cloak opens, D-13): the board's face. Pattern options below.
3. **Pockets** (seen in the lining): how each cell holds its component.

## Lining patterns
| Lining | Look | Earned |
|---|---|---|
| Chalk slate | Dark board, chalk tally marks (D-03 default) | Start |
| Star chart | Constellation lines between cells | Starwatch Standing |
| Ledger grid | Ruled paper, salt-stained | The Retort |
| Herbarium | Pressed leaves at the cell corners | Hedge Circle |
| Peat weave | Woven reed, bone toggles | Barrow Wardens |
| Chainmail mesh | Iron rings forming the grid | Anvil Hall |
| Road map | Faded routes running between cells | The Waymarch |

## Pocket styles
- Stitched pockets, glass jars, leather loops, small drawers, mesh nets.
- A component shows its own silhouette in the pocket; the pocket style is only the frame.

## Readability rules
- The **cell grid stays visible** whatever the lining: lining pattern sits at low contrast (2 palette steps from the base).
- Row letters A-F and columns 1-9 are always stitched in, in the same place ([[Board Label Lore]]).
- Addressed-cell glow and [[XOR Feedback]] colors are reserved; no lining may use them.

## Wear and patches
- The shell picks up **patches** from places: a salt-crusted hem after the Saltings, a moss patch after the Tanglewood. Patches are optional and can be removed.
- Finished storylines add a line of embroidery along the hem.
- Dyes apply to shell and lining separately ([[Component Dyes]]).
