# Familiar Customization

How your familiar looks and behaves. **Pact boons are progression** (D-08, [[Progression Axes]]): a Pact grants a **sense** (night sight, scent, tremor...). Species is a look only. This note covers looks and personality; a familiar's sense never depends on its species or accessories.

## Species (by where you meet them)
| Region | Species ideas |
|---|---|
| The Glasshigh | Owl, moth with star-dust wings |
| The Saltings | Salt eel, salt newt, hermit crab in a vial |
| The Tanglewood | Hedgehog, stag beetle, a small fox |
| The Barrowfen | Toad, crow, a lantern-wisp |
| The Ironreach | Ember salamander, a clockwork wren |
| The Crossings | River otter, heron |

- A species is a **look**. Any Pact's sense can be carried by any species; you choose which body your pact takes.
- Each school's storyline introduces its region's familiars: [[Astral Storyline]], [[Alchemy Storyline]], [[Bloom Storyline]], [[Necro Storyline]], [[Smith Storyline]], [[Wayfarer Storyline]].
- In iso a familiar is 8-16 px tall: color and silhouette carry it.

## Looks
- Color variants per species (from provenance and dyes, [[Component Dyes]]).
- Accessories: tiny hat, bell, satchel, lantern, ribbon, a matching rune tag.
- Each species has its own **vision tint** for [[Familiar Vision]] (owl = sepia, the prototype's default; salt eel = brine-blue shimmer; newt = green murk; crow = high-contrast grey).

## Personality (no stats)
| Trait | Shows as |
|---|---|
| Curious | Wanders to points of interest, sniffs components |
| Grumpy | Sighs at wrong casts, sits on your hat |
| Timid | Hides in the cloak during scenes |
| Showy | Mimics your spell's color, poses in the land wide shot |
- Personality changes idle animations and one-line barks in scenes ([[Cinematic Scenes]]), nothing else.
- Naming: free text.
