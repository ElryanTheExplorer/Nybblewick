# Sanctum Decor

How your sanctum in the Hollow looks. The stations' **numbers** (output, rates, upgrades) belong to [[Sanctum Stations]]; this note covers looks only. Part of the "wizard setup" answer for Q-26.

## What you can change
- **Furniture** (Loom, Wardrobe, lectern, dye vat, practice candle) is decor with a use: it doesn't count toward the station cap and generates nothing ([[Sanctum Stations]]).
- **Station skins:** each station keeps its silhouette (so it's recognizable) but swaps materials. A still can be copper (Alchemy), glass (Astral) or clay (Bloom).
- **Placement:** the sanctum floor is a tile grid like the board; decor snaps to tiles. Stations snap too, but their links live in [[Sanctum Stations]].
- **Lighting:** candles, star-lamps, ember bowls. Lit by casts, like Nybblewick's lamps.

## Trophies and provenance displays
- **Provenance shelf:** jars of notable components, each with a label: where, when, how ("Moonsilver, Glasshigh cairn, full moon, 3rd autumn"). Picks from [[Provenance]].
- **Gift wall:** items given by NPCs and friends, with the giver's name ([[Gift and Provenance Economy]]).
- **Map table:** a model of the Ring; restored regions turn green and lit ([[Restoration Loop]]).
- **Practice candles** (furniture): one candle per nybble; lit when you reach a [[Prompt Ladder]] rung for it. A visible record of fluency.
- **Order banners:** hung at Standing milestones ([[Standing]]).

## Who sees it
- Co-op guests walk through it ([[Drop-In Co-op]]); async visitors may see a snapshot ([[Community Layer]]).
- Visitors can leave one small token (a pressed flower, a chalk mark), kept with their name.

## Earning decor
- Same sources as [[Wizard Drip]]: story, gifts, crafting, restoration, seasons. No shop.
