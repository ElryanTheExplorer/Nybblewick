# Anti-Chore Rules

Rules every generator, timer, daily-type feature and seasonal event must obey. [[Sanctum Stations]], [[Automation]], [[Commission Loop]] and the [[Seasonal Contest Loop]] are checked against this list. The [[Not Doing]] note points here.

## The rules
1. **Absence never punishes.** Nothing you own decays, dies or is stolen while you are offline. Bloom freshness and other school clocks tick only in active play, and stores in the sanctum cellar are paused.
2. **Generators fill to a cap, then wait.** A station fills its tray to a cap and stops. What's in the tray is never lost.
3. **The cap is generous.** Proposed cap: about three play sessions of output, so checking once a week loses little.
4. **Collect in one action.** All station trays empty at the sanctum door in one interaction. No walking a circuit of buckets.
5. **No streaks, no login rewards.** Nothing grows because you came back on consecutive days.
6. **Tasks wait.** Commissions stack (up to six) and never expire. A town can post a new one, but old ones don't vanish.
7. **No real-world clocks.** No event requires play at a specific real hour. In-game day, night and seasons follow play time ([[Day Night and Seasons]]). **The one exception:** shared real-world **contest seasons** (one per quarter, ending in the one-week Wick Fair) for the [[Seasonal Contest Loop]] and [[Community Layer]]. Missing one costs nothing you already have.
8. **Seasonal content returns.** Contest cosmetics and seasonal recipes come back in a later cycle; nothing is "now or never". Late joiners get a catch-up multiplier on contributions.
9. **Story is never seasonal.** No storyline beat or lore is locked behind a contest or season, or given only to a contest's winner. Contest prizes are cosmetic or communal.
10. **Co-op never strands progress.** A guest's progress saves locally; a host leaving never undoes it.
11. **Welcome back, not catch up.** After a long absence, the game summarises what changed and offers a short re-fluency drill; it never presents a backlog of losses.

## Checklist for any new system
- Does it lose anything if the player is away for a month? (must be no)
- Does it reward logging in, or reward playing? (must be playing)
- Can a player who plays two hours a week enjoy it? (must be yes)
- Is there a cap, and is the cap stated in the UI?

**Recommendation:** adopt the list as a gate: a World or Sanctum note with numbers states which rules it satisfies.
