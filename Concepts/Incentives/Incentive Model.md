# Incentive Model

What makes Nybblewick rewarding without an economy (D-28). Six reward types, each fed by specific loops. **No currency** (the proposed answer to **Q-25**).

## Reward taxonomy
| Reward | What the player gets | Main loops |
|---|---|---|
| **Mastery** | Fluency: faster, cleaner casts; quicker duel tempos; Two-Handed Bytes | [[Light-Up Drill]], [[Duel Loop]], [[Chord QTEs]] |
| **Discovery** | New places, spells, secrets, glyph words, story branches | [[Expedition Loop]], [[Astral Scouting Loop]], [[Research Loop]] |
| **Expression** | Wizard drip, cloak look, sanctum decor, familiar looks | [[Sanctum Loop]], [[Wizard Drip]], [[Sanctum Decor]] |
| **Visible world change** | Lit lamps, mended bridges, grown forests that stay | [[Restoration Loop]], [[Commission Loop]] |
| **Social** | Gifts given and received, relit messages, witnessed duels, contest results, Standing | [[Ritual Raid Loop]], [[Seasonal Contest Loop]], [[Community Layer]] |
| **Capability** | New spells, cloak capacity, runes, pacts, stations | [[Story Scene Loop]], [[Cloak Pocket Progression]], [[Sanctum Stations]] |

Rule of thumb: every loop pays in **at least two** types, and at least one of them is not Capability. That keeps the game from becoming a stat ladder.

## Why no currency
- **Meaning:** a coin has no provenance. A moss from a friend's mended bridge does. Currency flattens every component into a price.
- **Grind:** currency invites farming the most efficient loop; the [[Not Doing]] list forbids grind economies.
- **Co-op health:** no currency means nothing to sell, so nothing to bot, dupe or trade for real money.
- **Scope:** no prices to balance, no sinks to tune, no inflation over seasons.
- Exchange still exists as gifting and barter ([[Gift and Provenance Economy]]).

## Under both Q-01 outcomes
| | Casts **consume** components | Casts only **require** components |
|---|---|---|
| Foraging pays in | Restock (Capability), plus quality and provenance | Variety and quality (new spells become possible) |
| Sinks | Casting itself, gifts, offerings | Gifts, contest offerings, ritual offerings, school behaviours (Alchemy charges, Bloom freshness, Smith heat) |
| Gifts feel | Generous (you give up casts) | Precious (you give up a spell option) |
| Risk | Chore-like restocking; needs [[Anti-Chore Rules]] caps | Hoarding; needs offering moments to move goods |

Either way, the rewards above hold; only the sink changes.

**Recommendation:** treat the reward taxonomy as a checklist on every loop note (the Loop template already has a "Rewards" line). Reject any loop that only pays Capability.
