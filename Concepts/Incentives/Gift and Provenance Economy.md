# Gift and Provenance Economy

Exchange without a market. Components move by **gifting** and **barter**, and their [[Provenance]] is what makes a gift mean something.

## What provenance records
- **Where:** region and spot ("the third barrow, Barrowfen").
- **When:** season and time of day ("spring, under a full moon").
- **How:** foraged, grown in a sanctum, won in a duel, kept from a co-op restoration, taken (stolen).
- **Hands:** up to three names it passed through ("found by Wren, given to Aldo, given to you").

## NPC preferences
Each named NPC has two or three **likes**, written as provenance, not item IDs:
- The ferryman at the Crossings: anything from the far bank, or found at dawn.
- A Barrow Warden: grave-wax from the barrows of her own family; dislikes anything raised without the land's leave.
- The Starwatch archivist: starglass found under the moon phase it was named for.

A gift's weight = **fit to a like** × **quality** × **story**. Quality is Plain, Fine or Storied ([[Component Taxonomy]]); it changes a component's state and story, **never its power** (first-hand beats re-gifted; a Keepsake from a friend's world beats a common find). Good gifts raise [[Standing]], unlock scenes and sometimes return a gift (notes toward a spell, a seed, a rune).

## Barter
- NPCs offer fixed **fair swaps** ("two salt pinches for a moss mat"). No fluctuating prices, no haggling minigame.
- Swaps are for convenience, not profit: you cannot chain them into a gain.

## Co-op exchange
- **Hand to hand:** in a [[Drop-In Co-op]] session, offer a component and the other player accepts. No price fields.
- **By post:** send a gift to a friend's world asynchronously ([[Community Layer]]); it arrives at their sanctum door.
- **Waystone gifts:** leave one for a stranger.

## No auction house
- No listings, no prices, no global market. Reasons: [[Incentive Model]] (meaning, grind, botting, scope).
- **Anti-farming:** NPCs value repeated gifts of the same thing less each time within a season; re-gifted items carry their chain, so laundering shows.
- Stolen items carry "taken from …" and NPCs dislike them ([[Subterfuge]]).

**Recommendation:** keep provenance **social**: it moves NPCs, Standing and story, not spell power. A place-resonance bonus (Tanglewood moss mending Tanglewood bridges better) stays only an option under Q-31, owned by the Spells notes.
