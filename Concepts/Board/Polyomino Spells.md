# Polyomino Spells

Spells are small polyomino pieces anchored at an **origin cell** (the one you chord).

- Bigger footprint = more power and more border for components.
- Rearranging happens out of combat. The first page is pre-arranged so optimizing is a mastery layer, not a gate.
- Rotation is the only manual fitting step.
