# Board Label Lore

Why A-F and 1-9? Undecided.

- **Margins:** coordinates printed in the grimoire's margins.
- **Kamea lineage** (lore lead, verify): Agrippa's *Three Books of Occult Philosophy* (1533) gives magic squares of order 3-9 for the seven classical planets (Saturn 3, Jupiter 4, Mars 5, Sun 6, Venus 7, Mercury 8, Moon 9). 6x9 is the Sun's side crossed with the Moon's.
- "Top finger open means letter" doesn't hold exactly: 8 and 9 also have that bit set.
