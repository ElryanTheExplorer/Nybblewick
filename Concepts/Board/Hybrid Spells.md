# Hybrid Spells

Need components from two schools on their border, like gold (multicolor) cards.

- Generic `any` sockets = colorless mana. Dual components = rare dual lands.
- Prototype examples: Brimstone rain (Alchemy + Smith), Stormstep (Smith + Wayfarer).
