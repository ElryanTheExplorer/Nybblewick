# Components

Foraged pieces with **tags** (school), **quality** and **shape**.

- Biomes act as basic lands; borderlands drop dual components. Your route decides your colors.
- Rare components are wildcards or add a tag.
- Per-school behaviors: Alchemy charges, Smith heat, Bloom freshness, Necro rot (see [[Schools MOC]]).
- Live in a bag or the [[Cloak Inventory]] (D-13).
