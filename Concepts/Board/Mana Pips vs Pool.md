# Mana Pips vs Pool

**Q-02:** Typed socket pips (`{Smith}{any}`), or a per-school mana pool? The prototype uses pips.

- Pips: spatial, visible, a packing puzzle.
- Pool: simpler, less tied to layout. Shared components could draw from a cluster pool, or hold their own charge, trading slots for burnout.
