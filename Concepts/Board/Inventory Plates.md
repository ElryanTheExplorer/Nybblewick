# Inventory Plates

Six inventory methods explored early. **Plate I preferred** (D-19).

| Plate | Method | Lineage | Layout skill | Hand skill | Yields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Packed grid | Backpack Hero, RE attaché case, Opus Magnum | High | Medium | shapes, tags |
| II | Linked sockets | PoE gems, Diablo runewords | Medium | Low | gems, supports |
| III | Wand tape | Noita, Magicraft, Zachtronics | High | Medium | tokens, modifiers |
| IV | Element codex | Magicka, Little Alchemy, Outer Wilds log | Low | High | fingers, discoveries |
| V | Page loadouts | Slay the Spire, Monster Hunter | Medium | Medium | recipes, ink |
| VI | Belt and bandolier | Dark Souls flasks, Zelda bottles | Low | Medium | charges |

Plates IV and VI are cheap and stack with anything. Plate IV survives as [[Finger Unlock Progression]].
