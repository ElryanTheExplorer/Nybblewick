# Cloak Inventory

Components live in a bag or inside the cloak; opening the cloak (animated) shows the slotted inventory (D-13).

## Proposals
- Cloak lining **is** the board: stitched pockets.
- Snap-in with a magnet pull, no physics.
- Two tiers: bag for bulk, cloak for the active loadout.
- Opening the cloak mid-fight = vulnerability window (not built).

## Concern
Realistic fitting is cool but complex and could break immersion.
