# Cloak Pocket Progression

How the board's **capacity** grows: the Cloak axis of the [[Progression Axes]] ([[Canon Sheet]]). Looks are separate ([[Cloak Appearance]]).

Two limits stack:
- **Reach:** which cells your fingers can address ([[Finger Unlock Progression]]).
- **Pockets:** which cells your cloak has sewn. A cell is usable only if it is both reachable and sewn.

## Reach: finger unlocks (the first hour)
Rows need the index finger plus the middle or ring finger, so the first pair is forced.

| Stage | Fingers | Rows | Columns | Reachable cells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | index + ring | A (`1010`) | 2, 8 | **2**: `A2`, `A8` (both cost 3) |
| 2 | + pinky | A, B | 1, 2, 3, 8, 9 | **10** |
| 3 | + middle | A-F | 1-9 | **54** |

Stage 1 needs exactly two spells, your origin's first Voice spells on `A2` and `A8` (the Style Room prototype used `A2` Blink, now Lodestep, and `A8` Sprout, [[Prototypes]]). Each stage is an apprentice lesson ([[Light-Up Drill]], [[Prompt Ladder]]): 1-bit, then 2-bit, then 4-bit numbers. This answers the open thread in Finger Unlock Progression: **index comes first, paired with ring**.

## Pockets: cloak tiers (the long term)
After stage 3 the hand reaches all 54 cells, but the cloak doesn't. Pockets are sewn in **4-cell patches** (any tetromino shape, placed where you choose), so the player decides where the board grows: usually toward cheap cells first ([[Finger Cost]]).

| Tier | Pockets | How earned | Also unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apprentice smock | 10 (the stage-2 cells) | Start | — |
| Travelling cloak | 22 (+3 patches) | First expedition; Waymarch or own-order gift. Comes pre-sewn for the Apprentice page ([[Loadout Presets]]); later patches are placed freely | A field **bag** for bulk components |
| Order mantle | 34 (+3 patches) | Mid-storyline [[Standing]] with your order | **Page 2** ([[Grimoire Pages]]) |
| Grimoire coat | 46 (+3 patches) | Late storyline, or Standing with a second order ([[Origins and Splashing]]) | **Page 3**; catalyst pockets glow |
| Full lining | 54 (+2 patches) | Restoration milestones and co-op rituals | — (preset slots come only from the Wardrobe furniture, [[Loadout Presets]]) |

Patch sources (no currency, [[Incentive Model]]): order gifts at Standing thresholds; a sanctum Loom station that sews a patch from Twine and a Storied component ([[Sanctum Stations]]); a thank-you from a restored town ([[Restoration Loop]]).

## Rules
- The **bag** has no shape puzzle and generous room. Only the cloak is a packing puzzle, so carrying never becomes inventory chores ([[Anti-Chore Rules]]).
- Pocket count never limits *knowing* spells, only *carrying* them. Your grimoire can hold every spell; your cloak holds the ones you bring.
- No tier requires speed (D-20). Fluency makes casts cheaper; pockets make the board bigger. They are separate axes.

## Fork
**Q-36:** After all four fingers, should pockets still gate the board, or should all 54 cells open at once?
- Gate (as above): a long, visible Cloak axis; layout stays a puzzle with scarcity.
- Open: simpler, and the 54-cell board *is* the reward for learning four fingers.

**Recommendation:** gate, but generously: 10 → 22 within the first two sessions, so the board never feels cramped while the player is still learning chords.
