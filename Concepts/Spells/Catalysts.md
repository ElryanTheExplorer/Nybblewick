# Catalysts

Rare components that change **what a spell does**, not how strong it is. A catalyst turns "light a patch" into "darken a patch", or "open a door" into "open it forever".

## Rules (Proposal)
- A catalyst applies only when it **borders the origin cell** (the cell you chord). This makes placement a real choice: the origin's neighbors are the most contested pockets on the board.
- A catalyst carries a school tag and can also fill a matching socket, but its twist applies whether or not it fills one.
- One catalyst per spell. Two bordering the origin → the one placed last wins (shown on the spell's card).
- Catalysts work on any spell whose verb they name, so one find can transform several spells in one school, and sometimes in a neighbor's.

## How catalysts are found
- **Events:** eclipses, lightning, migrations ([[Day Night and Seasons]]).
- **Story:** a gift at a branch point; the same scene can give a different catalyst by school ([[Story Scene Structure]]).
- **Standing:** orders hand them to trusted members ([[Standing]]).
- **Restoration:** healed places give back once ([[Restoration Loop]]).
- **Experiment:** a few appear only when two commons react in the [[Research Loop]].
Never from grinding a node: a catalyst always has a story ([[Gift and Provenance Economy]]).

## Under Q-01
**Q-32:** Is a catalyst consumed on use?
- Consumed: each one is a precious single cast (a "one-time permanent door").
- Kept: each one permanently changes a spell's behavior while placed.

**Recommendation:** **kept**, whatever Q-01 decides. Catalysts are already rare and story-bound; spending them punishes players for using their best find. If consumption is chosen, apply it only to catalysts whose twist is itself permanent (Twin keys: one permanent door per key).
