# Necro Storyline

**Order:** The Barrow Wardens, a barrow-hall in the Barrowfen.
**Mentor:** **Warden Ilse Marrow**. Quiet, funny at funerals, keeps a ledger of every name in every barrow; treats the dead as neighbors who happen to live underground.
**Inciting problem (tied to the land):** the dead of the Barrowfen have gone quiet. Ancestors no longer answer at the barrow doors, so Rot isn't raised and returned, the Mulch overflows, and the grey blight spreads into the Tanglewood. The Hedge Circle blames the Wardens.

Necro is **not a villain** ([[Not Doing]]). The Wardens are archivists and composters: they keep memory and return what has spoiled to the land.

## Arc
1. **Act I, The Silent Barrows:** you learn remembering and speaking with the dead by coaxing one old voice back: a ferryman buried with his oar. You learn the dead aren't gone; they've forgotten their own names.
2. **Act II, The Ledger:** the barrows' name-seals are worn. Without names, the dead can't answer. You must re-read the seals ([[Parity Inscriptions]], [[Serial Spirits]]). Meanwhile the Hedge Circle comes to accuse the Wardens ([[Bloom Storyline]]), and the Waymarch wants a road through the barrows.
3. **Act III, What We Keep:** (a) restore every name (slow; the dead return and Rot is raised again, the blight recedes); (b) let some names go so the land can take them back (faster; the Mulch heals, but some stories are lost forever); (c) give the dead new names from the living (the Wardens adopt the Hollow's families into the barrows, binding town and fen).

## Moral tension
Memory versus release. Remembering everyone is a burden the land can't carry; forgetting is a loss nobody can undo.

## Unlocks
- **Voice spells** (D-08): remember (replay what happened at a place), speak with the dead (a narrative branch, [[Spellbook as Vocabulary]]), raise Rot (return spoiled components to use), quiet a restless spirit. See [[Necro Spellbook]].
- **Runes:** name-seals ([[Parity Inscriptions]]), "bricked spirit" seals that need the right waking sequence ([[Rune Puzzles]]).
- **Pact:** the sense of **echo** (see the last thing a place remembers). Offered looks: barrow crow, moth, fen hare.
- **Places:** the Name Hall, the Mulch and Grave Forges borderlands, the ferryman's barrow.

## Ties to other schools
- **Shares Rot with Bloom** ([[Rot]], D-07): Act II meets the Bloom storyline in the Mulch.
- **Feeds Smith:** grave iron and the dead's memory of lost techniques.
- **Checks Wayfarer; checked by Alchemy:** barrows bar roads; Retort preserving keeps things from returning to the land. See [[Cross-School Tensions]].

## In each premise
- **The Hidden Machine:** Necro is memory and storage. The dead "forgetting their names" is corrupted address tables; Act III (b) is freeing memory, (c) is remapping it.
- **The Long Dusk:** the dead are the only ones who remember the world before the Dimming. Speaking with them is how lost spells come back.
- **The Wick Fair:** the Fair's Night of Lanterns, when the dead visit, is threatened. Act III is a gentle remembrance festival.

**Q-48:** can NPCs who were lost on a failure branch earlier in the game be met again as dead in the Barrowfen? It makes failure branches matter ([[Story Scene Structure]]) and gives Necro unique scenes, but multiplies writing.
