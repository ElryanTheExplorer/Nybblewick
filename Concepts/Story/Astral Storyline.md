# Astral Storyline

**Order:** The Starwatch, seated at the Starwatch Observatory in the Glasshigh.
**Mentor:** **Ysolde Fenn**, Keeper of the Lens. Precise, dry, half-blind from forty years at the eyepiece; reads the sky by memory now and hates admitting it.
**Inciting problem (tied to the land):** the stars over the Glasshigh no longer match the Starwatch's charts. Constellations drift a hand's width a season. Nybblewick's lamps, timed by the stars, light late or not at all, and moor travellers are getting lost.

## Arc
1. **Act I, the Wrong Sky:** you learn mapping and light by re-charting the moors at night. You find the drift is real, and it is not uniform: one quarter of the sky is "late". Ysolde refuses to publish, because a wrong chart would shame the order.
2. **Act II, the Old Orrery:** at **the Old Orrery**, a ruin reached by the Orrery Stair, you rebuild the orrery whose seal is a [[Gray Code Locks|Gray code lock]]. It shows the sky as it *should* be. Ysolde wants to project that sky back over the moors as an illusion so nobody panics. The Waymarch, whose ferrymen navigate by stars, demands the truth.
3. **Act III, Show or Shade:** you can (a) reveal the true sky and help the ring adjust, (b) hold up Ysolde's illusion while you search for the cause, or (c) find the cause first, which needs help from a neighbor order. Each changes how the Glasshigh looks for the rest of the game.

## Moral tension
Truth versus comfort. Astral is the school of light and illusion: when is it kind to show people a prettier sky than the real one?

## Unlocks
- **Voice spells** (via this storyline, D-08): chart a region, hang a lamp that stays, stage an illusion over a place, read a telegraph before it happens. Names and cells in [[Astral Spellbook]].
- **Runes:** [[Gray Code Locks]] (the Old Orrery), constellation seals that open when components form the right pattern.
- **Pact:** the sense of **night sight** ([[Familiar Vision]]). Offered looks: moor owl, nightjar, moth.
- **Places:** the Old Orrery (via the Orrery Stair), a dark-sky camp on the Glasshigh, the Star Roads borderland opened for travel.

## Ties to other schools
- **Feeds Alchemy:** the Retort needs star timing for ripening; a late sky spoils their batches. A shared Act II scene in the Starsalt Flats.
- **Fed by Wayfarer:** ferrymen bring sightings from the river plains; the Waymarch is the first ally in Act II.
- **Checks Bloom; checked by Smith:** Astral lamps keep the Tanglewood from sleeping (Hedge Circle complaint); forge smoke from the Ironreach clouds the eyepiece. See [[Cross-School Tensions]].

## In each premise
- **The Hidden Machine:** the sky is the machine's clock and display. The drift is clock skew; the "late quarter" is a failing oscillator. Act III (c) is the first time a player sees the sky as rows of hex.
- **The Long Dusk:** the sky dims, not drifts. The Starwatch keeps a few true stars "lit" by ritual. Act III asks whether to spend the order's last Starglass on the moors or on Nybblewick.
- **The Wick Fair:** the Fair's opening is timed by a star that rose late; the Starwatch is blamed. Act III is a star-show at the Fair: honest charts, or a crowd-pleasing illusion.
