# Alchemy Storyline

**Order:** The Retort, a guild-house on the Saltings.
**Mentor:** **Mother Agathe Sallow**, Mistress of the Pans. Warm, exacting, keeps every recipe in her head so nobody can steal it; smells permanently of sulfur and lemon balm.
**Inciting problem (tied to the land):** the Saltings' brine pans have stopped crystallizing. The hot springs run sour, the salt comes out grey, and without good salt the Hollow cannot preserve its winter food.

## Arc
1. **Act I, Grey Salt:** you learn brewing and preserving by saving one village's winter stores with what poor salt there is. Tasting, timing, ripening ([[RC Ripening Curves]]). You trace the sourness upstream to a sulfur vent that has woken.
2. **Act II, The Secret Book:** the cure exists, in a recipe Agathe swore never to write down. The vent can be dissolved and the springs cured, but only at scale, with help from the Hedge Circle's moss and the Starwatch's timing. Sharing the recipe means the Retort loses its monopoly.
3. **Act III, Open or Sealed:** (a) publish the recipe to the ring and let other orders brew it; (b) keep it secret and have the Retort do all the curing (slower, the Retort grows powerful); (c) a middle way: teach it only to those who swear an [[Notoriety and Oaths|Oath]].

## Moral tension
Knowledge as a commons versus knowledge as a craft that must be protected. A cure that anyone can brew can also be brewed badly.

## Unlocks
- **Voice spells** (D-08): brew a reaction, preserve (stop a freshness clock), dissolve a blockage, cure a sickness in people or land. See [[Alchemy Spellbook]].
- **Runes:** dilution seals (voltage dividers as alchemical dilution, [[Rune Puzzles]]), [[Parity Inscriptions]] on recipe tablets.
- **Pact:** the sense of **taste-at-a-distance** (read what is dissolved in water or air). Offered looks: salt eel, avocet, marsh newt.
- **Places:** the Sour Spring vent, the Retort's still-room, the Starsalt Flats and Fungal Springs borderlands.

## Ties to other schools
- **Fed by Astral:** star-timed ripening; the Starwatch's drift ruins batches (see [[Astral Storyline]]).
- **Feeds Bloom:** salts and tinctures are the Tanglewood's fertilizer; a Fungal Springs scene where Bloom asks for a cure the Retort hasn't shared.
- **Checks Necro; checked by Wayfarer:** preserving stops Rot, so the Barrow Wardens see the Retort as hoarding the dead's due; Waymarch canals drain brine pans and open roads leak recipes. See [[Cross-School Tensions]].

## In each premise
- **The Hidden Machine:** Alchemy is the analog layer: slow curves and thresholds. The sour vent is a component drifting out of tolerance. The secret recipe is a calibration table.
- **The Long Dusk:** the springs are cooling, not souring. The recipe is the last one from before the Dimming; publishing it is the first act of trust between orders since.
- **The Wick Fair:** the Retort's famous Fair cordial tastes wrong this year. Act III is whether to share the cordial recipe as the Fair's gift.
