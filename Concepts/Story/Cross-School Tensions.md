# Cross-School Tensions

The [[Rivalry Ring]] read as story. Ring order from the [[Canon Sheet]]: **Astral → Alchemy → Bloom → Necro → Smith → Wayfarer → Astral**.

## Feeds and checks (Proposal)
Two cycles, borrowed in shape from the five-phase cycle (verify): each school **feeds** its clockwise neighbor and **checks** the school two steps ahead. Neighbors are allies with friction; checks are where real disagreements live.

| School | Feeds (clockwise neighbor) | Checks (two ahead) |
|---|---|---|
| Astral | Alchemy: star timing for ripening | Bloom: lamps keep the wood from sleeping; calendars dictate planting |
| Alchemy | Bloom: salts, tinctures, fertilizer | Necro: preserving keeps things from returning to the land |
| Bloom | Necro: ripeness spoils into Rot | Smith: roots crack forges; forests resist charcoal-cutting |
| Necro | Smith: grave iron, the dead's lost techniques | Wayfarer: barrows bar roads; memory binds people to place |
| Smith | Wayfarer: nails, rails, magnetized lodestones | Astral: forge smoke clouds the sky |
| Wayfarer | Astral: sightings, observers, star roads | Alchemy: canals drain brine; roads leak recipes |

The opposite pairs (Astral-Necro, Alchemy-Smith, Bloom-Wayfarer) have no direct relationship: they meet only through others, so their scenes are rare and odd ("what does an astronomer say to a barrow-warden?").

## Flashpoints between neighbors
Each borderland hosts one flashpoint scene, played in both neighbors' storylines.

| Borderland | Flashpoint | The choice |
|---|---|---|
| Starsalt Flats | late stars spoil the Retort's batches | publish the drift or protect the Starwatch's name |
| Fungal Springs | Bloom needs a cure the Retort won't share | trade the recipe for Standing, or brew it in secret |
| The Mulch | grey blight; who is to blame | tend together, cut, or wall it off ([[Bloom Storyline]], [[Necro Storyline]]) |
| Grave Forges | Smiths dig grave iron from old barrows | ask the dead, or take it |
| Stormline | store the storm or route it (Storm hook, Q-04) | [[Smith Storyline]], [[Wayfarer Storyline]] |
| Star Roads | ferrymen need true stars; the Starwatch shows an illusion | truth now, or comfort until it's fixed |

## Splashing pulls you into two stories
When you splash a second school ([[Origins and Splashing]]), its storyline opens at Act I, and every flashpoint between your two schools becomes a **divided-loyalty scene**: you have Standing with both orders and both expect you to side with them.
- Splashing a **neighbor** puts you in the middle of one flashpoint. You get a third option only someone with both schools' verbs can cast (a hybrid branch).
- Splashing a **checking** school (two apart) is harder in story: the orders distrust each other, and your second order's mentor tests your loyalty early.
- Splashing the **opposite** school gives you the rare "bridge" scenes, which unlock nothing mechanically but reveal the most lore (in P1, how two subsystems that never touch are linked).

## Mixed schools in co-op
- Story scenes are **host-led**: the host's storyline is the chapter being played ([[Drop-In Co-op]]).
- Each guest's school adds its verbs to the scene. A party with Smith and Wayfarer can take the hybrid Stormline branch even if the host has neither.
- At a flashpoint, the **host decides**. Guests' casts add options: a guest casting for "their" order puts that order's branch on the table, and players casting together can open a hybrid branch ([[Choir Casting]]).
- Guests earn Standing in the host's world for what they did there; their own storyline does not advance.

**Q-46:** should a guest's own storyline advance when they play the matching chapter in a host's world? Options: (a) never, host only (simplest); (b) the guest gets a "witnessed" credit that skips that scene's setup in their own world; (c) full shared progress. Lean (b).
