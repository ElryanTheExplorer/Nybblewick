# Wayfarer Storyline

**Order:** The Waymarch, a ferry-station in the Crossings.
**Mentor:** **Pell Ashby**, ferrywoman. Talks to her ropes, never sits down, has crossed the river more times than anyone alive and keeps a tally of it on her oar.
**Inciting problem (tied to the land):** the waystones of the Crossings have started pointing wrong. Lodestones swing after every storm, carts end up in the wrong valley, and a ferry went downstream with a family aboard and landed somewhere no map shows.

## Arc
1. **Act I, Lost Roads:** you learn ferrying and tethering by bringing the lost family home. You find the misdirection is worst near the Ironreach ridges, where lightning strikes the old roads.
2. **Act II, The Stormline:** at the Smith-Wayfarer seam, lightning re-magnetizes lodestones. Pell wants to move the roads away from the storms. The Anvil Hall wants to build lightning rods along the roads to harvest the storms. Either way the old roads, and the barrows they pass, are disturbed.
3. **Act III, Where the Road Goes:** (a) move the roads (safe, but cuts towns off from each other and from the Barrowfen); (b) ride the storm: learn to travel *on* the current with the Smiths (the first hybrid travel spell); (c) open the old Waydoors early: the fixed doors to Nybblewick that restoration opens ([[Transport Network]]), skipping the roads entirely at the cost of the ferries' purpose.

## Storm hook (Q-04)
The Stormline is where Storm lives. This storyline and the [[Smith Storyline]] meet there in one shared chapter, seen from two sides: Wayfarer wants to *route* the storm, Smith wants to *store* it. Act III (b) is a Smith + Wayfarer hybrid (Stormstep in the prototype, [[Hybrid Spells]]). **Recommendation:** keep Storm a seam, not a school; its story is the one place two orders must share a verb. See [[Storm]].

## Moral tension
Connection versus place. Every road you open carries people, goods and trouble; every door you open makes someone's ferry or village unnecessary.

## Unlocks
- **Voice spells** (D-08): Lodestep (a short blink), Tether, Lay road, ferry across water, pull a component (magnetism). Player-placed doors come later, through the Twin keys catalyst ([[Catalysts]]). See [[Wayfarer Spellbook]].
- **Runes:** via-and-layer seals ([[PCB Seals]]: a via is a portal between layers), routing puzzles.
- **Pact:** the sense of **current-feel** (where water and roads lead). Offered looks: river otter, heron, ferry cat.
- **Places:** the Stormline borderland, the Star Roads, the Crossings Waydoor (to Nybblewick), the lost valley from Act I.

## Ties to other schools
- **Fed by Smith:** nails, rails, magnetized lodestones; the Anvil Hall builds what the Waymarch routes.
- **Feeds Astral:** ferrymen carry sightings and observers to the Glasshigh.
- **Checks Alchemy; checked by Necro:** canals drain the Saltings, roads spread Retort secrets; roads cross barrows, and the Barrow Wardens refuse passage. See [[Cross-School Tensions]].

## In each premise
- **The Hidden Machine:** Wayfarer is routing and addressing. Lightning flips bits in lodestones (single-bit errors, cf. [[Parity Inscriptions]]). Doors are direct jumps to an address.
- **The Long Dusk:** roads are the Ring's veins and most are broken. Act III decides how the rebuilt Ring is wired.
- **The Wick Fair:** the Fair guests can't find the Hollow. Act III picks a permanent route for future Fairs.
