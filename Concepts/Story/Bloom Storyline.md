# Bloom Storyline

**Order:** The Hedge Circle, a grove in the Tanglewood.
**Mentor:** **Old Tamsin Hawe**, hedge-witch. Barefoot, blunt, unhurried; grafts apple trees onto anything that stands still; believes every problem is a season, not a crisis.
**Inciting problem (tied to the land):** a grey blight is creeping out of the Mulch (the Bloom-Necro borderland) into the Tanglewood. Leaves go soft and pale overnight; the Hedge Circle's younger members blame the Barrow Wardens.

## Arc
1. **Act I, The Grey Edge:** you learn growing and mending by holding the blight's edge: replanting, grafting, moving seedlings. You find the blight is Rot that nobody has tended: the Mulch is full of spoiled growth that should have been raised or returned.
2. **Act II, The Wardens:** you go into the Barrowfen to accuse the Wardens and find them overwhelmed: the dead have gone quiet ([[Necro Storyline]]), so the Rot is not being raised. Tamsin's rivals in the Circle want to burn the Mulch with Smith fire. Tamsin wants to let it run its course.
3. **Act III, Tend or Cut:** (a) work with the Wardens to restore the Rot cycle (slow, shared, Bloom and Necro bound together); (b) cut a firebreak with the Anvil Hall (fast, loses part of the wood forever); (c) terraform a new boundary: raise a living hedge-wall that holds Rot in the Mulch (keeps the wood, but the Mulch becomes a wound both orders must tend).

## Moral tension
Growth versus decay as one cycle. Bloom must accept that its own ripeness feeds Rot, and that the Wardens are partners, not polluters.

## Unlocks
- **Voice spells** (D-08): grow (seed and ripen), mend (living things and wood), *Upheave* (raise ground, shift a stream), *Root bridge*, *Greening*. See [[Bloom Spellbook]].
- **Runes:** [[RC Ripening Curves]] seals (ripen to the exact threshold), graft-knot seals.
- **Pact:** the sense of **scent** (smell Rot and ripeness through soil). Offered looks: hedgehog, badger, truffle pig.
- **Places:** the Grey Edge, the Hedge Circle's grafting yard, the Mulch and Fungal Springs borderlands.

## Ties to other schools
- **Shares Rot with Necro** ([[Rot]], D-07): the Bloom and Necro storylines meet in the Mulch in Act II and must choose Act III together if both are played.
- **Fed by Alchemy:** salts and tinctures; the Retort's secret cure ([[Alchemy Storyline]]) could heal the blight faster.
- **Checks Smith; checked by Astral:** roots crack forges and forests resist charcoal-cutting; Starwatch lamps keep the wood from sleeping. See [[Cross-School Tensions]].

## In each premise
- **The Hidden Machine:** Bloom is the growth rules (think cellular automata). The blight is a rule stuck in one state because the memory subsystem (Necro) stopped clearing it.
- **The Long Dusk:** the Tanglewood is one of the few places still growing; the blight threatens the last green. Act III (c) is a lasting monument.
- **The Wick Fair:** the blight eats the Fair's orchard. Act III decides whether the harvest happens this year.
