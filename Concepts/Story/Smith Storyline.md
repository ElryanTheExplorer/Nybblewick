# Smith Storyline

**Order:** The Anvil Hall, a forge-town in the Ironreach.
**Mentor:** **Hob Calloway**, ironmaster. Big, gentle, deaf in one ear from the hammers; names every tool he makes; believes anything broken can be made better than new.
**Inciting problem (tied to the land):** the Ironreach forges keep overheating and cracking. Storms over the ridges have grown stronger and strike the forge-town every week; the iron itself holds charge and won't let it go.

## Arc
1. **Act I, Cracked Anvils:** you learn forging and repairing by mending the town's broken tools and its bell. Heat and overheat are your clock ([[Smith]]). You find the iron is charged because the storms are feeding the hills.
2. **Act II, The Rods:** Hob wants to stop the storms; his rival, Forgewright **Senna Dray**, wants to harvest them with lightning rods along the old roads and power every station in the Hollow. The Waymarch says the rods are why lodestones point wrong ([[Wayfarer Storyline]]).
3. **Act III, Bank the Fire:** (a) harvest the storm (Hollow stations gain power; the Stormline stays dangerous to travel); (b) ground it (storms calm; Smith loses a power source); (c) share it with the Waymarch: a storm-road where Smith stores the current and Wayfarer rides it (the Stormline chapter).

The **Lightning Spire** ritual ([[Encounter Types]], [[Ring Regions]]) follows your Act III choice: (a) catch the storm into the Anvil Hall's banks; (b) ground it through the Spire; (c) split it between the forge banks and the storm-road.

## Storm hook (Q-04)
Smith and Wayfarer play the same Stormline chapter from two sides (see [[Wayfarer Storyline]]). Smith wants to **store** the storm, Wayfarer to **route** it. Storm stays a seam; its spells need both schools' components ([[Hybrid Spells]], Struck lodestone).

**Q-49:** If a player has only one of the two schools, how much of the Stormline chapter can they finish? Options: (a) all of it, with an NPC from the other order doing the other half; (b) up to the choice, then a co-op or splash is needed for (c). Lean (a): no story should be locked behind co-op ([[Design Pillars]] 5).

## Moral tension
Power versus restraint. The storm is free energy that hurts travellers and hills; Smith's craft is to make things better, but "better" for whom?

## Unlocks
- **Voice spells** (D-08): forge (shape metal), repair (mend any made thing), power (charge a station or mechanism). See [[Smith Spellbook]].
- **Runes:** decoupling and power seals ([[PCB Seals]]: caps next to the power pin, or the spell browns out), "magic smoke" failure seals.
- **Pact:** the sense of **heat sight** (see warmth and charge in metal). Offered looks: forge salamander, iron beetle, soot cat.
- **Places:** the Bell Tower, the Stormline borderland, the Grave Forges borderland, Senna's rod-field.

## Ties to other schools
- **Fed by Necro:** grave iron and the dead's forge-lore; the Barrow Wardens remember techniques the Anvil Hall lost.
- **Feeds Wayfarer:** nails, rails, magnetized lodestones.
- **Checks Astral; checked by Bloom:** forge smoke clouds the Glasshigh's eyepiece; the Tanglewood resists charcoal-cutting. See [[Cross-School Tensions]].

## In each premise
- **The Hidden Machine:** Smith is the power subsystem. The storms are surges; the forges are failing regulators. (c) is installing a proper power rail.
- **The Long Dusk:** the forges are the Hollow's only heat in a cooling world. Grounding the storm means a colder winter.
- **The Wick Fair:** the Fair needs a new bell; the storm keeps cracking it. Act III picks the bell's metal and story.
