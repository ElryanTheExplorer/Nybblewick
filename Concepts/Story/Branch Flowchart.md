# Branch Flowchart

After each chapter, a flowchart of every branch is revealed, drawn as **the flowchart leaf** in your grimoire (Detroit's chapter flowchart as reference, [[Reference Deconstruction]]).

## The flowchart leaf
- Ink on parchment. Each **node** is a decision point or a [[Chord QTEs|chord QTE]] beat, drawn as a small sigil. A QTE beat forks three ways: clean, late or wrong, each its own authored branch.
- **Taken paths** are inked solid. **Untaken paths** are faint pencil lines.
- **Locked nodes** (never reached) are ink blots with a hint glyph on top.
- **End states** are drawn as the image the chapter left behind (a lit lamp, a burned hedge).
- The leaf is bound into the grimoire beside the board pages ([[Grimoire Pages]]), so story and spells live in one book. ("Page" means a board page only.)

## Hinting hidden branches
A blot shows *what kind* of thing opens it, never the exact answer:
- **School sigil:** a verb from that school would open it ("a Necro mark here").
- **Hybrid sigil:** two school marks overlaid; needs both (a splash or a co-op partner).
- **Rune mark:** a seal you haven't learned yet.
- **Standing mark:** an order's banner; you lacked their trust.
- **Fuse:** a clean QTE was needed (the QTE timer is a fuse, [[Chord QTEs]]).
- In P1 ([[Premise Options]]), node IDs are printed in hex in the margin. Late-game, players can read them.

## Replay value
- **Rehearsal** (Proposal): replay any node from the leaf as a dream (Astral flavor). You see the branch play out, the leaf fills in, nothing in the world changes.
- **Rewrite** is not part of normal play: branches stand. It exists only as an **accessibility option** under Q-42 ([[Story Scene Structure]]), letting a player retry a beat for real.
- Completion per chapter shown as filled nodes (e.g. 7 / 12), not a percentage bar.
- Async community stats (what % of players chose each branch) shown after the chapter, from [[Community Layer]].

**Q-43:** when do the blots appear? Options: (a) only after the chapter ends (Detroit-like; no spoilers); (b) live during the chapter as you pass them (helps discovery, risks paralysis); (c) a setting. Lean (a), with blots shown live only in Rehearsal.

## Teaching tie
The leaf is quietly a decision tree, and in P1 a state diagram. It teaches branching logic the way the board teaches hex ([[Binary to Hex Teaching]]).
