# Community Ritual Conflict

Community vs community, as ritual rather than twitch combat.

- Rival towns contest a season, a river or a harvest with big slow spells. Preparation is the fight.
- Sieges are huge slow wardings or unwardings ([[Choir Casting]]).
- Spectators can read the hand signs.
- Built-in cost: spells draw on land you'd be harming.
- The [[Rivalry Ring]] is the diplomatic map.
