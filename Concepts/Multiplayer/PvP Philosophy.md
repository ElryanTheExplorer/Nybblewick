# PvP Philosophy

Player-vs-player exists, but as ritual, consent and cost, never as the core. This is a **refinement of D-21**, not a reversal: combat is still not the game; a duel is a formal, rare, rule-bound conversation between two wizards.

## Why it is frowned upon
- Combat needs fluency the game doesn't demand (D-20). PvP against a fluent player is the fastest way to make a beginner quit.
- The world fiction treats wizard-on-wizard harm as a breach: magic draws on the land, so a fight spends land you share.
- It breaks pillar 5 if it allows free griefing ([[Not Doing]]).

## Why it still exists
The designer wants a pull toward subterfuge and the occasional clash. Three forms, each with consent built in:

| Form | Consent | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| [[Formal Duels]] | Both accept a challenge and its terms | Wagers, time, Standing if dishonourable |
| [[Subterfuge]] in shared sessions | The host's **Hearth Rule** allows it; everyone sees the rule before joining | [[Notoriety and Oaths\|Notoriety]] if caught; stolen goods carry provenance |
| [[Seasonal Contest Loop]] | Pledging to a town for the season | Land drawn on, components offered |

## Hearth Rules (host setting, shown before joining)
- **Peaceful** (default): no subterfuge; duels only by challenge.
- **Mischief**: illusions, disguises and pranks allowed; no theft or sabotage.
- **Cutpurse**: full subterfuge; caught acts add Notoriety.

## "Necessary" cases
When PvP is the honest answer, not griefing:
- **Disputes in co-op:** two players want opposite outcomes at a contested site (raise the barrow, or seal it). The players may *agree* to settle it by duel. The host never forces a duel: by default the host decides, and guests' casts add options ([[Drop-In Co-op]]).
- **Calling a thief to account:** a caught subterfuge can be answered with a challenge the thief cannot refuse without gaining Notoriety.
- **Oath of the Witness:** the one [[Notoriety and Oaths|Oath]] that obliges you to accept duels.
- **Community contests:** seasonal rivalry between towns.
- **Story duels:** NPC rivals in school storylines (single-player practice for the form).

## The costs, always
- Nothing a player has *learned* can be lost: no spells, board pages or notes, no fluency, no Standing taken by another player directly.
- Only **wagered** components, favours or keepsakes change hands.
- Unconsented acts are always visible afterwards (Notoriety, provenance tags).

**Recommendation:** default every session to Peaceful and make the first duel a story duel, so players meet the form in a safe place.
