# Subterfuge

The sanctioned way to be sneaky: illusions, disguises, theft, sabotage and misdirection, inside shared sessions whose Hearth Rule allows it ([[PvP Philosophy]]) and inside community contests. It is never free: it leaves traces, and caught acts add [[Notoriety and Oaths|Notoriety]].

## The verbs
| Act | How (school lean) | Allowed under |
|---|---|---|
| **Illusion** | **Glamour** (Astral): a fake lamp, a false path, a hall dressed to look abandoned | Mischief, Cutpurse, contests |
| **Disguise** | Glamour works on places, so a disguise needs a **catalyst variant** of Glamour that dresses a person ([[Catalysts]]): look like another player or a local NPC for one scene | Mischief, Cutpurse |
| **Theft** | **Lodepull** (Wayfarer): pull one item out of an unwarded stash or stockpile. (Pocket swap only moves your own pieces, so it can't steal.) | Cutpurse, contests (stockpiles only) |
| **Sabotage** | Overheat a rival's forge (Smith), spoil an Alchemy stock, **blight a Bloom offering** (Necro) | Cutpurse, contests |
| **Misdirection** | False glyph messages, fake star charts, a redirected ferry (Wayfarer) | Contests, the [[Community Layer]] (rated by readers) |

## Necro cancelling Bloom (D-07)
- Necro raising drains freshness from living neighbours ([[Rot]]). In a contest, a Necro player can turn a rival town's Bloom offering to Rot.
- It is not pure destruction: Rot is a resource, so the rival can **compost** it back, or their own Necro players can raise it. Sabotage shifts the land's balance; it doesn't delete work.
- Done openly under contest rules it is a **Blight Gambit**, a legitimate move. Done covertly in a co-op session, it is sabotage and adds Notoriety.
- Necro stays not-a-villain: Bloom has the mirror move (overgrowing a Necro barrow-offering until it can't rot).

## What makes it worth doing
- **Scarce rares:** a meteor fall in a shared session drops one meteoric iron. Who gets it?
- **Contest wins:** a stolen stockpile or a well-timed Blight Gambit can swing a [[Seasonal Contest Loop]].
- **Secrets:** a disguised wizard can sit in another order's scene and learn a branch clue from their storyline.
- **Heist commissions:** NPCs ask for something back ("The Retort took our ancestor's urn"), turning theft into story ([[Commission Loop]]).

## Counterplay
- **Wards** (Salt-and-star ward, Briar hedge round a stash) stop theft outright.
- **Astral divination** and **Unweave** see through and break Glamours, and divination reads the residue of recent theft (who, roughly when).
- **Familiars** as watchmen: a familiar left on a stash reports anyone who touches it ([[Familiar Vision]]).
- **Provenance:** stolen components carry a "taken from …" line that NPCs read. Stolen goods are poor gifts.
- **Calling to account:** a caught thief can be challenged to a duel they can't refuse without another Notoriety step ([[Formal Duels]]).

**Recommendation:** ship illusion and disguise (Mischief) first; they are funny, harmless and show off Astral. Add theft and sabotage only with contests.
