# Notoriety and Oaths

**Oaths** are opt-in vows that grant a boon while kept. **Notoriety** is the visible mark left by broken oaths and caught subterfuge. Together they make honesty a choice with rewards, and mischief a choice with a reputation. ([[Standing]] itself, your relationship with orders and towns, is a Story/RPG system; this note only says how oaths and Notoriety touch it.)

## Oaths
Swear at an order's seat or a waystone. Up to **three** active oaths at once. Oaths also appear in story: in the [[Alchemy Storyline]], the Retort's cure can be taught only to those who swear an Oath.

| Oath | Vow | Boon while kept |
|---|---|---|
| Oath of the Open Hand | Never steal or sabotage | NPCs offer a gift-share on first visits; stashes count as warded in Cutpurse sessions |
| Oath of the Lamp | Relight every dark lamp you pass | Your lantern lights a wider radius; lamps you light stay lit through bad weather |
| Oath of the Witness | Accept every duel challenge | Duel wagers you win carry a rarer provenance line |
| Oath of Plain Sight | Cast no illusion or disguise | Astral divinations around you show more (illusions near you are obvious to your party) |
| Oath of the Barrow | Return all Rot you raise to the land | Barrow Wardens share stories of the dead earlier in your storyline |
| **Oath of the Order** | Keep your order's charter (each order writes its own: the Retort's is "share no recipe unsworn") | The only route to **Sworn** [[Standing]]: the order's deepest secret and a boon |

- Breaking an oath ends the boon, costs Standing with the order that heard it, and adds one Notoriety step.
- Swearing again needs a short penance (below).

## Notoriety: the lantern
Every wizard carries a **lantern** (hat lantern, staff or hood light, see [[Wizard Drip]]). Notoriety changes how it burns.

| Step | Lantern | NPCs | Players |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Clear | Steady flame | Normal | Normal |
| 1 Smoking | Thin smoke trail | Gift-givers more guarded; order clerks note your name | Visible on the join screen |
| 2 Guttering | Flickers, burns blue | Some commissions withheld; back-road barterers in the Crossings start talking to you | Hosts can bar Guttering guests |
| 3 Snuffed | Dark lantern, relit only by a ritual | Town lamplighters refuse to light your way; one order seals its doors | Cannot join Peaceful sessions as a guest |

- Notoriety also **opens** content: a rogue path of back-road barterers and heist commissions (no markets; they swap, they don't sell). Being notorious is a story, not a ban.
- Notoriety never takes away knowledge, fluency or components.

## Redemption
- **Penance deeds:** a restoration job chosen by the wronged order ([[Restoration Loop]]).
- **Return the goods:** provenance shows what you took; returning it clears that act.
- **The Relighting:** a public ritual at Nybblewick with a witness (player or NPC) that steps your lantern back by one.
- **Q-63:** Should Notoriety also fade on its own over in-game seasons, or only through deeds?

**Recommendation:** deeds-only, and never by real-world time. Absence should neither punish nor launder ([[Anti-Chore Rules]]).
