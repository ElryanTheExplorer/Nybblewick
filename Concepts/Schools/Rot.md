# Rot

A resource shared by [[Bloom]] and [[Necro]]. Bloom components lose freshness and become rot; Necro consumes rot and drains living neighbors.

- In the [[Utility-First Pivot]], rot becomes an ecological force: blight that Bloom and Necro tend or fight ([[The Land's Moods]]).
- Could be a literal capacitor discharge curve ([[RC Ripening Curves]]).
