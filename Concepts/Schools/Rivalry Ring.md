# Rivalry Ring

Five schools in a ring, each feeding one neighbor and checking another (like the MTG color wheel, or the five-phase cycle).

- Suggested pie: Astral, Alchemy, Wayfarer, Bloom, Smith. Necro needs a slot or becomes a Bloom hybrid (Q-05). Storm is the Smith-Wayfarer seam (Q-04).
- Doubles as the **diplomatic map** of communities ([[Community Ritual Conflict]]).
