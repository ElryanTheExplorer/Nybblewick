# Astral

**Verb:** Pattern

**Mechanic:** components form constellation shapes for bonuses (prototype: two or more astral contributors in one row or column = x1.5 power). Moon phase or time of day can charge them. Divination = information, e.g. previewing telegraphs.

**Utility identity:** mapping, lighting, stagecraft, illusions that change how a place feels.

**Lore leads (verify):** astrologer, Persian magus, Chaldean sky-reader

Covers light of any kind, folding in illusion and divination.
