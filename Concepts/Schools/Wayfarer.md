# Wayfarer

**Verb:** Motion

**Mechanic:** teleport swaps two cells; magnetism pulls components. The one school that rearranges the loadout mid-cast.

**Utility identity:** ferrying, tethering, doors between settlements; logistics as magic.

**Lore leads (verify):** shamanic soul-flight, Daoist step patterns, the witch's mortar, hoodoo lodestone

The locomotion school (teleportation, maybe magnetism).
