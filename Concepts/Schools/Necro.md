# Necro

**Verb:** Raise

**Mechanic:** shares one resource with [[Bloom]]: [[Rot]]. Bloom ripens until components spoil; Necro raises the spoiled. Raising drains freshness from living neighbors.

**Utility identity:** remembering; the dead hold the stories, so speaking with ancestors doubles as narrative branching.

**Lore leads (verify):** volva, sangoma

Raising dead plants steals nourishment from living plants instead of giving it. Golgari (black-green) as reference.
