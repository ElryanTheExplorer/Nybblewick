# Progression Axes

Voice, Pact and Rune are **not schools** (D-08). They are ways in:

- **Voice:** storylines that teach spells.
- **Pact:** familiars as boons ([[Familiar Vision]]).
- **Rune:** writing seals to unlock things ([[Rune Puzzles]]).
