# Smith

**Verb:** Charge

**Mechanic:** resistive heating: current heats metal, metal carries current. Metal soaks charge or heat across casts and releases it. Overheating is its clock (prototype: overheat at 3 heat for 4.5 s).

**Utility identity:** forging, repairing, powering.

**Lore leads (verify):** Ogun (Yoruba orisha of iron), Mande blacksmith caste and nyama, Norse and Yakut smiths

Fire and electricity combined. See [[Storm]].
