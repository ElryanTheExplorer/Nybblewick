# Bloom

**Verb:** Growth

**Mechanic:** components seed into neighbors, ripen, then rot. Freshness is a clock (prototype: -1 every 3 s, becomes [[Rot]] at 0).

**Utility identity:** growing, mending, terraforming.

**Lore leads (verify):** druid, curandera, rootworker

The plants school. Complements and can cancel [[Necro]].
