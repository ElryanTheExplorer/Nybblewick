# Cast Flow

1. Chord the row, commit.
2. Chord the column, commit. The spell readies if its sockets are at least partly filled.
3. Mouse aims. Left click uses; right click zooms (maybe).
4. Esc or thumb stows.

Mechanics differ per spell ([[Aim Archetypes]]). Familiar-assisted interactions were floated; flagged as scope creep.
