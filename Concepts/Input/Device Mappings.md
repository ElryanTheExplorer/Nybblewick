# Device Mappings

| Device | Thumb | Index → pinky |
|---|---|---|
| Keyboard, right | `b` | `7 8 9 0` |
| Numpad, right | `0` | `7 8 9 +` |
| Drumpad, right | pad 1 | pads 9, 14, 15, 12 |
| Drumpad, left | pad 4 | pads 12, 15, 14, 9 |

Prototype assumptions (not decided): left keyboard = space + `4 3 2 1`; left numpad mirrored; space aliases thumb everywhere; drumpad keyboard fallback `zxcv / asdf / qwer / 1234`; MIDI pad 1 = note 36.
