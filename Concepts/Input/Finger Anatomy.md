# Finger Anatomy

The ring finger is hardest to move alone because it shares tendons with its neighbors, so ring-only chords feel expensive.

- Gives [[Finger Cost]] a body-level layer.
- Accessibility: some players can't do some shapes at all. Remappable bits per finger may be needed.
