# Rune Puzzles

Rune puzzles can be embedded-engineering puzzles: PCB layout, binary, hex and other easter eggs (D-22). Rune is also a [[Progression Axes|progression axis]] (writing seals to unlock things).

## Written up

## More seeds (Proposals, not yet split out)
- **Floating pins:** an unconnected input is an unbound spirit until pulled up or down.
- **Debounce:** a rune that chatters and needs a time filter.
- **Voltage dividers:** alchemical dilution.
- **Resistor color bands:** read the rings on a charm.
- **Watchdog timers:** a ward that resets the area if you don't pet it in time.
- **Interrupts vs polling:** a guardian that sleeps until woken, or stays awake burning power.
- **Endianness:** a seal that only works read in the right byte order.
- **LFSRs:** looks random but cycles; the key is the seed.
- **Memory maps:** the cloak as a memory map; alignment, overlap, overflow.
- **Magic smoke:** miswire and the smoke escapes, a visible fizzle.
- **Bricked spirits:** unresponsive until reflashed with the right bootloader sequence.
