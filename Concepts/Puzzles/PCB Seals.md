# PCB Seals

Seals as circuit boards: route traces with no crossings, match lengths so two runes arrive together, keep sensitive runes away from noisy ones.

- Ground plane = warding circle.
- Via = portal between layers ([[Wayfarer]]).
- Decoupling caps next to the power pin, or the spell browns out.
