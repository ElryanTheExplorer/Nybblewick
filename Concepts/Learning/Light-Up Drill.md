# Light-Up Drill

A board square lights up and the game says "cast it". You chord its address. That's the whole loop.

## Why start here
- Smallest possible test of the chord feel, before inventory, schools or story.
- Teaches the board, the hand and hex at once.
- Produces data: per-nybble speed and error rate ([[Finger Cost]], measured).

## Variants (Proposals)
- **Prompt style:** show the cell, the hex (`C7`), the binary (`1100 0111`), the decimal (`12, 7`), or only the spell name ([[Prompt Ladder]]).
- **Feedback:** on a miss, light the fingers that were wrong ([[XOR Feedback]]).
- **Pacing:** untimed practice, then a metronome, then a rhythm track.
- **Spaced repetition:** serve the nybbles you're slowest at more often.
- **Fiction:** an apprentice's lesson, a candle that lights per correct cast (the "wick" in Nybblewick), a familiar that points at cells.
- **Trigger:** test [[Cast Trigger Modes]] here; auto-detect may suit drills best.
