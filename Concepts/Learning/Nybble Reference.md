# Nybble Reference

`|` = open finger (1), `.` = closed (0). Order: index, middle, ring, pinky.

| Hex | Dec | Binary | Hand | Role on board |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | 0000 | `. . . .` | fist; not a column |
| 1 | 1 | 0001 | `. . . \|` | column 1 |
| 2 | 2 | 0010 | `. . \| .` | column 2 |
| 3 | 3 | 0011 | `. . \| \|` | column 3 |
| 4 | 4 | 0100 | `. \| . .` | column 4 |
| 5 | 5 | 0101 | `. \| . \|` | column 5 |
| 6 | 6 | 0110 | `. \| \| .` | column 6 |
| 7 | 7 | 0111 | `. \| \| \|` | column 7 |
| 8 | 8 | 1000 | `\| . . .` | column 8 |
| 9 | 9 | 1001 | `\| . . \|` | column 9 |
| A | 10 | 1010 | `\| . \| .` | row A (wild column → 2) |
| B | 11 | 1011 | `\| . \| \|` | row B (wild → 3) |
| C | 12 | 1100 | `\| \| . .` | row C (wild → 4) |
| D | 13 | 1101 | `\| \| . \|` | row D (wild → 5) |
| E | 14 | 1110 | `\| \| \| .` | row E (wild → 6) |
| F | 15 | 1111 | `\| \| \| \|` | row F, open hand (wild → 7) |
