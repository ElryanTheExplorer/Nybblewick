# Pitch

**Nybblewick is a wizard RPG where you cast by speaking with your hand: four fingers, four bits, one spell.**

## The fantasy
You are an apprentice who learns magic as a language. At first you spell every word slowly; by the end you speak in whole sentences, and the land answers.

## What you do
- **Chord** spell addresses (row, then column) from a grimoire you arranged yourself ([[Two-Chord Addressing]], [[Scopeless Board]]).
- **Forage** components across a ring of regions and **pack** them into your cloak to power spells ([[Components]], [[Cloak Inventory]]).
- **Change the world**: light the dark, raise roads, grow forests, mend bridges, speak with the dead ([[Utility-First Pivot]]).
- **Follow your school's story**, meet the other orders, and choose what the land becomes ([[Story and RPG MOC]]).
- **Build your sanctum** in the home valley; its stations grow components while you travel ([[World MOC]]).
- **Join friends** for great rituals that need many hands ([[Multiplayer MOC]]).

## Why it's different
- The input *is* the magic system, and fluency in it is the progression.
- It quietly teaches binary and hex ([[Binary to Hex Teaching]]).
- Preparation and expression matter more than reflexes ([[Design Pillars]]).

## Look and feel
Low-res pixel isometric 3D for exploration, cinematic scenes for story moments, first person inside the coat for duels ([[Art Direction]], [[Presentation MOC]]).

## Premise (pick one later, see [[Premise Options]])

- **The Long Dusk**: a faded world rebuilt with utility magic.
- **The Wick Fair**: cozy seasons in a lamp-lit town.
