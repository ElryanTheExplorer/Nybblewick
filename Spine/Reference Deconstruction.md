# Reference Deconstruction

What to **take** from each touchstone, what to **leave**, and how it translates. These are mechanic references only; Nybblewick copies none of their worlds, characters or look.

## Factorio (automation, logistics)
- **Take:** throughput puzzles; automation that unlocks scale; your base as visible progress.
- **Leave:** combat pressure on the base; endless sprawl; punishing complexity.
- **Translation:** small "enchanted automation" in the Hollow. [[Sanctum Stations]] feed each other via [[Transport Network]] and Smith power ([[Automation]]).

## Baldur's Gate 3 (systemic RPG, origins, co-op story)
- **Take:** systemic reactivity (spells interact with the environment and each other); origin characters with personal quests; utility solutions in dialogue; co-op through a story game.
- **Leave:** turn-based tactical combat as the main loop; dice deciding outcomes; a cast-of-thousands scope.
- **Translation:** school storylines as origins ([[Origins and Splashing]]); casting *in* conversation; fluency instead of dice.

## Elden Ring (discovery, async community)
- **Take:** unguided discovery and landmarks; interconnected regions; async messages and co-op summons; rest sites as places to change your build.
- **Leave:** difficulty as identity; reflex combat; punishing death loops.
- **Translation:** messages written as chord or hex glyphs ([[Community Layer]]); waystones for loadout swaps ([[Loadout Presets]]); scouting by [[Astral Projection]].

## Detroit: Become Human (cinematic choice)
- **Take:** a branch flowchart revealed after each chapter; QTEs woven into scenes; consequences that persist; framing that mixes third person and cinematic shots.
- **Leave:** no multiplayer (we keep co-op outside story scenes, or host-led); a fully hand-authored cinematic budget.
- **Translation:** [[Chord QTEs]] (the [[Light-Up Drill]] becomes the QTE engine); the [[Branch Flowchart]] as a grimoire page; [[Cinematic Scenes]].

## Magic: The Gathering (color identity)
- **Take:** schools as identities with friends and rivals; hybrid costs; mono-school payoff.
- **Leave:** the card economy and collection grind.
- **Translation:** [[Rivalry Ring]], [[Hybrid Spells]], [[Devotion]], borderland duals ([[Canon Sheet]]).
