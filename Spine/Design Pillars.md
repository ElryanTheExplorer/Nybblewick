# Design Pillars

Five filters. A feature earns its place by serving **at least one** pillar and breaking none of the [[Not Doing]] rules.

## 1. Casting is speaking
The chord is a language you become fluent in. Fluency is the main skill and the main "level".
- **Passes if** it gives chords more to say, rewards fluency, or teaches a nybble.
- **Fails if** it asks for speed before the game has taught it ([[Fluency as Skill]]).

## 2. The land answers
Spells change the world, and the change stays: light stays lit, roads stay built, forests stay grown.
- **Passes if** a cast leaves a visible, persistent mark, or the world reacts systemically.
- **Fails if** a spell only moves a number.

## 3. Preparation is power
Choosing components, arranging the cloak and planning a ritual matter more than reflexes.
- **Passes if** the best moment happens *before* the action ([[Polyomino Spells]], [[Sockets and Components]]).
- **Fails if** a twitch reaction decides the outcome.

## 4. Story through schools
Your school is your lens: its order, its storyline, its view of the land.
- **Passes if** a school changes what you can see, say or do in a story.
- **Fails if** schools differ only in damage type.

## 5. Together, mostly
Solo-first, with drop-in co-op. Shared rituals are the social peak. Conflict between players is consensual, ritual and costly (D-27).
- **Passes if** a second player makes it richer without being required.
- **Fails if** it lets one player grief another for free.
