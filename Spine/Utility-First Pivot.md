# Utility-First Pivot

Dissuade combat (D-21). Lean into utility spells, story and choose-your-own-adventure, player customization, environment customization. PvE first; combat mainly between communities.

Reason: the prototype feels clunky and combat is hard until fluent (D-20).

## Utility verbs per school (Proposal)
- [[Astral]]: map, light, stage, illusion
- [[Alchemy]]: brew, preserve, dissolve, cure
- [[Wayfarer]]: ferry, tether, open doors
- [[Bloom]]: grow, mend, terraform
- [[Smith]]: forge, repair, power
- [[Necro]]: remember, speak with the dead
