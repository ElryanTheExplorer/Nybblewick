# Nybblewick

A wizard RPG where spells are cast with **hand-sign nybbles**: four fingers make four bits, two chords make an address, and the thumb sets it off. It also quietly teaches binary and hex.

Previously called *Chorded Grimoire*. See [[Name - Nybblewick]].
